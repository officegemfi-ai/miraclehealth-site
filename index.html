House rules

What we won’t do.

Our rules, and Sig’s.

  • Sell you anything

    No shop. No ads. No affiliate links. Nobody here tells you where to buy, and posts that try are taken down.

  • Tell you a dose

    Not for anything, ever. The right amount depends on you and what else you take. That’s a conversation for your doctor.

  • Hide the sources

    Every claim links to where it came from. How each score is worked out is public too.

  • Let the numbers be gamed

    Copied posts, repeats and sudden floods of reports are held out. Points are for taking part, never for what you say happened.

In the community, members agree to: no talk of where to buy or sell; say what you did, not what others should do; be honest, even when it did nothing; adults 18 and over, one account each.

Questions

Straight answers.

Something else? Write to hello@miraclehealth.us

Is this medical advice?

No. It’s published research and people’s experiences, always labeled as which is which. Talk to your doctor before you start, stop or change anything.

Do you sell anything?

No. There is nothing to buy here: no shop, no ads, no affiliate links. Posts that name sellers or say where to buy are taken down.

Where do the numbers come from?

The studies come from FDA documents, NIH fact sheets and published trials, and each claim links to its source. The Community Signal is read from public forums for now, and shifts to members’ own reports as they add up. How the numbers are made walks through every step.

Why won’t you tell me a dose?

Because the right dose depends on you, your health and what else you take. For many of these there is no tested dose in people at all. That’s a conversation for your doctor, and Sig can help you write the questions to bring.

What happens to what I share?

You sign in to the community with your phone number, and nobody else ever sees it. Only signed-in members can read posts, unless you choose to make one of your reports public. You can delete your account and everything you wrote at any time. Sig’s chat needs no account, and we don’t store what you write there. We don’t sell your information, show ads or use tracking cookies. Read the privacy notice.

Is it free?

Yes. Reading the Library, asking Sig and joining the community are all free. The community is for adults 18 and over.
