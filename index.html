answerchecked by the safety agent

1

// the bottom linerecommender

No trial has tested HGH and enclomiphene together for fat loss. They act on two different hormone systems, growth hormone and testosterone, so any effect would come from each one separately.

2

// the evidenceevidence librarian

  • HGHadults with growth hormone deficiency, 6 months against placebo: less body fat and waist, more lean massstrong
  • HGHhealthy older adults, 18 small trials: about 2 kg less fat and 2 kg more lean mass; weight unchangedmoderate
  • Enclomiphenemen with secondary hypogonadism, randomized: testosterone, LH and FSH rose; FDA found no meaningful gain in symptomsmoderate
3

// evidence through relativesrelatives agent

Testosterone, which enclomiphene raises: about 1.6 kg less fat and 1.6 kg more lean mass in middle-aged and older men (29 randomized trials). Relatives show a likely direction; they aren’t results for these two.

4

// what people reportthe community

No member reports on the pair yet. On public forums, HGH has a Signal of 78.8 out of 100, mostly for recovery; neither one has reports for fat loss.

5

// good to knowsafety agent

HGH is FDA-approved for specific conditions, such as growth hormone deficiency. Enclomiphene isn’t FDA-approved, and US and European regulators have flagged blood clots.

6

// interpretation · efficacy onlyinterpretation agent

plausible, unproven

house rules · ours and sig’s

What we won’t do.

house-rules.md4 rules · 0 exceptions

  1. Sell you anything

    No shop. No ads. No affiliate links. Nobody here tells you where to buy, and posts that try are taken down.

  2. Tell you a dose

    Not for anything, ever. The right amount depends on you and what else you take. That’s a conversation for your doctor.

  3. Hide the sources

    Every claim links to where it came from. How each score is worked out is public too.

  4. Let the numbers be gamed

    Copied posts, repeats and sudden floods of reports are held out. Points are for taking part, never for what you say happened.

In the community, members agree to: no talk of where to buy or sell; say what you did, not what others should do; be honest, even when it did nothing; adults 18 and over, one account each.

Is this medical advice?

No. It’s published research and people’s experiences, always labeled as which is which. Talk to your doctor before you start, stop or change anything.

Do you sell anything?

No. There is nothing to buy here: no shop, no ads, no affiliate links. Posts that name sellers or say where to buy are taken down.

Where do the numbers come from?

The studies come from FDA documents, NIH fact sheets and published trials, and each claim links to its source. The Community Signal is read from public forums for now, and shifts to members’ own reports as they add up. How the numbers are made walks through every step.

Who are Sig’s twelve agents?

Sig is the AI behind Ask Sig. Its agents each do one job: the Safety agent reads every question first and checks every answer last; the Planner turns a goal into what studies measure; the Recommender reads the fact sheets and writes the answer; the Evidence librarian grades each study; the Relatives and Mechanism agents say what close relatives and biology suggest, marked as such; the Combination agent and the Synergy auditor look at what’s taken together; the Markers agent names blood tests worth asking about; the Stack builder lays out options to discuss; the community brings in what people report. The Interpretation agent reasons it all through, for efficacy alone, last.

Why won’t you tell me a dose?

Because the right dose depends on you, your health and what else you take. For many of these there is no tested dose in people at all. That’s a conversation for your doctor, and Sig can help you write the questions to bring.

What happens to what I share?

You sign in to the community with your phone number, and nobody else ever sees it. Only signed-in members can read posts, unless you choose to make one of your reports public. You can delete your account and everything you wrote at any time. Sig’s chat needs no account, and we don’t store what you write there. We don’t sell your information, show ads or use tracking cookies. Read the privacy notice.

Is it free?

Yes. Reading the Library, asking Sig and joining the community are all free. The community is for adults 18 and over.
