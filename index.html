An illustration of the layout, not any compound’s real numbers.

  1. 01

    Rated by use

    People take the same thing for different reasons. Each use, from joint pain to sleep, gets its own rating from first-hand reports once three people name it.

  2. 02

    One overall rating

    65% is what people report happened, 15% how much the reports agree, 10% how many independent voices there are and 10% how much there is to go on.

  3. 03

    Hard to game

    New accounts count half, and points go to taking part, never to results: a report that says it didn’t work earns exactly what one that says it did.

  4. 04

    Private where it matters

    Your phone number is never shown. Nobody sees who voted, who said “same for me”, or who reported whom.
