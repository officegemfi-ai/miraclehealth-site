// How it works

From one person’s report to everyone’s number.

  1. 01

    Share how it went

    What you took it for, how long, and whether things were better, the same or worse than before you started. Reports that say it did nothing, or caused side effects, count too.

  2. 02

    Miracle AI reads it

    Is it first-hand? Did it go better, the same or worse, for how long, with any side effects? You can confirm or correct that read on your post, and your word wins.

  3. 03

    The Signal moves

    The compound’s number is recalculated in the database as reports come in, and every change is kept as a new version.

// Fair by design

Nobody has a reason to tilt the numbers.

  • Points for taking part, never for results

    A report that says it didn’t work earns exactly what one that says it did.

  • New accounts count half

    So a wave of fresh accounts can’t push a number around.

  • Private where it matters

    Your phone number is never shown. Nobody sees who voted, who said “same for me”, or who reported whom.

// House rules

Experiences, not medical advice.

Miracle AI reads every new post and comment against the rules, and holds anything that likely breaks one for a moderator. Members can report anything, and moderators read every report.

  • No sourcing, no selling

    Nobody asks or tells where to buy, names sellers, or offers anything.

  • What you did, not what others should do

    Share your own experience. Nobody here is anyone’s doctor.

  • Be honest

    Including when something did nothing. One person, one account.

  • Adults only

    Members are 18 or older.
