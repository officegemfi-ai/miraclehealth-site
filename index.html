// House rules

What we won’t do.

Our rules, and Sig’s.

  • Sell you anything

    No shop, no sellers, no ads and no affiliate links. Nobody here says where to buy, and posts that try are taken down.

  • Tell you a dose

    Not for any compound. The right dose depends on you and what else you take, so it’s a conversation for your prescriber.

  • Hide the sources

    Every claim links to where it came from, and how every Signal is made is public.

  • Let the numbers be gamed

    Copies, repeat posts and sudden bursts of reports are held out. Points go to taking part, never to results.

In the community, members agree to: no sourcing or selling; share what you did, not what others should do; be honest, even when something did nothing; adults 18 and over, one account each.

// Questions

Straight answers.

Something else? Write to hello@miraclehealth.us

Is this medical advice?

No. It’s published research and people’s experiences, always labeled as which is which. Talk to a clinician before you start, stop or change anything.

Do you sell anything?

No. There’s nothing to buy here: no shop, no ads and no affiliate links. Posts that name sellers or say where to buy are taken down.

Where do the numbers come from?

The studies come from FDA documents, NIH fact sheets and published trials, and each claim links to its source. The Community Signal is read from public forums for now, and shifts to members’ own first-hand reports as they add up. How the numbers are made walks through every step.

Why won’t you tell me a dose?

The right dose depends on you, your health and what else you take, and for many of these compounds there’s no tested dose in people at all. That’s a conversation for your prescriber. Sig can help you get ready for it with a list of questions to bring.

What happens to what I share?

In the community you sign in with your phone number, and nobody else ever sees it. Posts are visible only to signed-in members, unless you choose to let anyone read one of your reports, and you can delete your account and everything you wrote at any time. Sig’s chat needs no account, and we don’t store what you write there. We don’t sell your information, show ads or use tracking cookies. Read the privacy notice.

Is it free?

Yes. Reading the Library, asking Sig and joining the community are all free. The community is for adults 18 and over.
