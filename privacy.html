Privacy notice

Effective November 10, 2026

Miracle Health (“we”) runs the Miracle Health community at community.miraclehealth.us and this website, including Ask Sig, our AI chat, at miraclehealth.us/sig. This notice explains what we collect, why, who helps us run the community, and the choices you have. Questions go to hello@miraclehealth.us.

The short version

  • You sign in with your phone number. Nobody else ever sees it.
  • What you post about your health is stored, shown to other signed-in members under your handle, and read by Sig to apply the rules and count toward the Community Signal. A report goes on a public page only if you choose that for it.
  • We don’t sell your information, show ads, or use tracking cookies.
  • You can delete your account, and everything you wrote, at any time.
  • Sig’s chat on this website needs no account, and we don’t store what you write there.
  • Check-in texts and the weekly email come only if you ask for them, and stop when you say so.

What we collect

Your account. Your phone number (to send sign-in codes), your handle, the invite code if you joined with one, your confirmation that you’re 18 or older, the version of the community rules you accepted, and when you agreed to how your health posts are handled.

What you share. Posts, comments, votes, “same for me” taps, stickers, the spaces you join, and the reports you file about posts that may break the rules. Posts are about your own experience with peptides, so they can include health information: what you took, why, for how long, what changed and any side effects.

Sig’s read of your posts. For each post: whether it’s a first-hand report; whether things got better, stayed the same or got worse; for how long; any side effects; and whether it may break a rule. You can confirm or correct the read on your post.

Your activity in the community. Points, emblems, badges and notifications, and moderation records (what was reviewed, held or removed, and why).

Your choices about reaching you. If you turn on check-in texts: that you did, when, your time zone (so texts come at a sensible hour) and which texts we sent. If you sign up for the weekly email: your email address, where on the site you signed up, when you confirmed, which issues we sent you and, if you unsubscribe, when.

Technical information. Like most online services, the companies that host the community keep short-lived logs of requests, including IP addresses and browser details, for security and to keep the service running. The app keeps your sign-in session in your browser’s storage. To show how strong the evidence is in each space, the app downloads the Library’s public index from miraclehealth.us, the same file for everyone. We don’t use advertising or analytics cookies.

How we use it

  • To run your account and the community: sign-in, posts, votes, notifications, points and emblems.
  • To build the Community Signal: one number per compound, calculated from many members’ reports. The number never shows who contributed.
  • To keep the community safe: applying the rules (Sig’s read, member reports and moderators’ decisions) and stopping spam, fake accounts and abuse.
  • To answer you when you write to us.

We don’t sell your information, use it for advertising, or share it with data brokers.

Who sees what

  • Other members see your handle, posts, comments, emblem and badges. Posts and comments are visible only to signed-in members, not to the public.
  • Anyone can read a report you choose to make public (“Let anyone read it”): its title, your words, your handle, what you took and how it went, on its own page at community.miraclehealth.us/r/, where search engines can find it. Comments on it stay members-only. Take it back any time and its page comes down within the hour; a report a moderator holds or removes, or that you delete, comes down the same way. Copies others made while it was public, or that search engines kept, are beyond our control.
  • Nobody sees your phone number, or who voted, who said “same for me”, who sent a sticker, who joined which space, or who reported whom.
  • Moderators see reports, posts held for review and Sig’s reads, so they can enforce the rules.

Companies that process it for us

  • Supabase hosts the community’s database and sign-in.
  • Twilio sends your sign-in codes, and check-in texts if you turn them on, so it receives your phone number and those texts.
  • Resend sends the weekly email and its confirmation emails, so it receives your email address and those emails.
  • Anthropic runs Claude, the model behind Sig. Each new post and comment is sent to it to check against the rules and to read the outcome, and so is each message in Sig’s chat on this website, to write the answer. Anthropic’s commercial terms don’t allow it to train its models on this content.
  • GitHub hosts these web pages and the community’s app files.

They use the information only to provide their service to us. We may also disclose information when the law requires it, or to protect someone’s safety.

Ask Sig, the AI chat on this website

Anyone can talk with Sig, Miracle Health’s AI, at miraclehealth.us/sig, without an account. Sig is an AI, built on Anthropic’s Claude, not a person. It’s for adults 18 and older.

  • Your messages. Each message you send, together with the earlier messages in the same chat, goes through our server to Anthropic so Claude can write the answer, drawing on the public Library and the Community Signal. We don’t store your messages or the answers, and our server doesn’t log what they say.
  • On your device. Your chats are kept in your browser’s local storage on that device, listed in Sig’s side panel, so they’re there when you come back. Nothing is sent to us to keep. They stay until you delete them, one at a time or all at once (“Delete all chats”), or clear this site’s data in your browser; Sig keeps your 60 most recent and lets older ones go to make room. Anyone who uses this browser on this device can see them. Which chats are open as tabs is kept only in that browser tab. If you confirm you’re 18 or older, your browser remembers that on this device.
  • What we count. To keep the free chat from being overused, we count how many messages each visitor sends in ten minutes and in a day. The count is kept under a code made from your IP address with a random secret that changes every day and is deleted after two days. Your address itself isn’t stored, and once the secret is deleted, nobody, us included, can turn the code back into your address or link it to other days. Old counts are deleted along with the secret, when the first message of a new day comes in.
  • At Anthropic. Anthropic processes the messages to provide the service to us, may not train its models on them, and by default deletes them within 30 days; content its safety systems flag can be kept longer to enforce its usage policy.
  • What you tell Sig. Sig doesn’t ask you questions. It answers with what you choose to tell it, such as how your nutrition, sleep and training look, the medicines you take or your health conditions, and says what would change its answer. What you tell it is handled like the rest of your messages: kept with your chats on your device and not stored by us. Its last agent, the Interpretation agent, reads the same messages and the public Library to weigh how well something may work; it sees nothing else about you.
  • Clinician packets. Sig no longer makes them. A packet link made before November 5, 2026 still opens: the packet travels inside the link itself (the part after “#”, which browsers don’t send to any server), so we never receive it, and anyone who has the link can read it.
  • Your saved stack. If you choose “Save on this device”, the compounds in your stack and the medicines you mentioned are kept in your browser’s local storage on that device, so they’re there next time. Nothing is sent to us. When you come back, your browser compares them with the public Library to show what has changed. Choose “Forget it” to delete them. A link to your saved stack carries the stack inside the link itself (after “#”), so we never receive it, and anyone with the link can read it.
  • Your Miracle Plan. Sig can lay out your plan as a picture: your foundations, what you take and the supplements and habits it suggests. If you share it, the plan travels inside the link itself, so we never receive it, and anyone with the link can see it. You can leave your peptides and medicines out before you share. “Save image” draws the picture in your browser; nothing is sent to us. If you choose “Keep on this device”, the plan and the steps you tick off are kept in your browser’s local storage on that device, so you can check in on it later; nothing is sent to us, and “Forget it” deletes it. Tapping where you are on a “not sure yet” step of the plan sends it to Sig as your next message, like typing it.
  • My Miracle and your check-ins. My Miracle (miraclehealth.us/my) shows what you keep on a device together: your plan, your saved stack and your check-ins. When you say how it’s going (better, about the same or worse, what you’re taking now and anything you add), the check-in is kept in your browser’s local storage on that device; nothing is sent to us. To show your compounds’ evidence and Community Signal, your browser downloads the same public files for everyone (the Library’s index and the community’s snapshot), so nothing tells us which compounds are yours. “Post it in the community” opens the community’s new-post form with a draft of your check-in in the link after “#”, which isn’t sent to any server; it’s only posted if you sign in and press Post there, and then it’s handled like any community post. “Ask Sig what’s next” opens Sig with the question typed in; it’s only sent if you send it. “Move it to your phone” makes a link and QR code that carry your plan, stack and check-ins after “#”, so we never receive them, and anyone with the link can read them. “Forget everything on this device” deletes all three.
  • Keep it general. Please don’t include your name, contact details or anything else that identifies you. You don’t need them to get a useful answer.

Check-in texts

Off unless you turn them on (in the community, Account, then Check-in texts). Then, when one of your reports is due for a check-in (2, 6 and 12 weeks after you post it), we text the number you sign in with: at most one text a day, between 9am and 8pm your time. The text never says what you took. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to stop them, or turn them off in Account; reply HELP for help. We never share your number for anyone’s marketing.

The weekly email

If you give us your email address for The Signal, we email you once to confirm it, and send nothing else until you do. If you don’t confirm within 30 days of that email, we delete your address. Once confirmed, you get one email a week, each with a one-click unsubscribe link. When you unsubscribe, we keep your address only to make sure it’s never emailed again; ask us and we’ll delete it entirely. To stop the sign-up form being misused, we count sign-ups from each connection in a day the same way as Sig’s chat: under a code made from your IP address with a secret that changes daily, never the address itself. We never sell or share your email address.

How long we keep it

For as long as your account exists. When you delete your account, your profile, posts, comments and votes are deleted right away. Reports you filed about other members’ posts stay, without your name. Backups and the hosting companies’ logs expire on their own schedules.

Your choices

  • Correct the read. Confirm or correct Sig’s read on any of your posts.
  • Public or not. Make a report public, or members-only again, on the report itself.
  • Texts and email. Turn check-in texts off in Account or reply STOP; unsubscribe from the weekly email from any issue.
  • Edit or delete your posts and comments at any time.
  • Delete your account in the app (Account, then Delete my account). This removes everything you wrote.
  • Ask us at hello@miraclehealth.us for a copy of your information, to correct or delete it, or to withdraw your consent. Depending on where you live (for example California, Washington or Nevada), the law gives you specific rights over your personal and health information. We honor these requests for everyone.

Adults only

The community is for people 18 and older. We close accounts that look like they belong to someone younger.

Security

Connections are encrypted (HTTPS), the database’s access rules let each member reach only what they’re allowed to, and sign-in uses one-time codes. No system is perfectly secure; if something goes wrong that affects your information, we’ll tell you.

Not medical advice

Miracle Health is a place to share experiences. Nothing here is medical advice: talk to a clinician before you start, stop or change anything. In a crisis, call or text 988 (US).

Changes to this notice

If we change this notice, we’ll update the date at the top and tell members in the community before significant changes take effect.
