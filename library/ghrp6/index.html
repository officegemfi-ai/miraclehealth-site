What it isevidence librarian

GHRP-6 is a lab-made peptide of six amino acids, two of them in mirror-image form (D-form). It came out of 1977 work on enkephalin-like (opioid-like) peptides and was the first growth hormone-releasing peptide with significant activity in the body. It acts on the ghrelin receptor to prompt the pituitary gland to release growth hormone, and it can briefly raise cortisol. It is not FDA-approved.

Limited evidence

Small, short human studies show growth hormone release (weaker in obesity), brief rises in cortisol and changes in sleep. There are no trials of health outcomes.

What it may doevidence librarian

  • Limited evidence

    Raised growth hormone release in adults with obesity, though less than in lean adults, alone or combined with GHRH 6

  • Limited evidence

    More stage 2 sleep with higher growth hormone in a small study of 7 healthy young men; other forms tested showed no sleep effect 6

  • Limited evidence

    Higher IGF-1 in 14 men on testosterone therapy who took it with GHRP-2 and sermorelin 7

Risks and side effectssafety agent

  • Not known

    FDA cites possible immune reactions from aggregation and impurities, plus concerns about cortisol and higher blood sugar from reduced insulin sensitivity 1

  • Not known

    Brief rises in cortisol and ACTH (stress hormones) 6

  • Not known

    Increased appetite and fat gain, which a review calls built into how ghrelin-type drugs work 8

  • Not known

    No long-term, cancer or mortality safety data exist for growth hormone secretagogues 6

  • Not known

    Black-market supply: a review reports ghrelin mimetics are sold on the black market, so identity and purity are uncertain 8

Who should skip it, or ask a doctor first

  • Diabetes or prediabetes (doctor first: may reduce insulin sensitivity)
  • Active cancer (no cancer safety data)
  • Pregnant or breastfeeding (no safety data)
  • Competitive athletes (prohibited by WADA at all times)

The main studiesevidence librarian

  1. Review2018Review of small human studies, including 19 adults with obesity and 7 healthy young men

    The Safety and Efficacy of Growth Hormone Secretagogues

    Describes GHRP-6 as the first growth hormone-releasing peptide with significant activity in the body. Small studies showed a blunted growth hormone response in obesity and diabetes, brief cortisol rises and more stage 2 sleep. 6

  2. Case series201714 men on testosterone therapy (retrospective chart review)

    Growth Hormone Secretagogue Treatment in Hypogonadal Men Raises Serum Insulin-Like Growth Factor-1 Levels

    A combination of GHRP-6, GHRP-2 and sermorelin raised average IGF-1 from about 160 to 239 ng/mL; body composition was not assessed. 7

  3. Review2012Review of published research

    Growth Hormone Doping in Sports: A Critical Review of Use and Detection Strategies

    Describes GHRP-6 as a non-natural, enkephalin-derived hexapeptide; ghrelin mimetics have moderate effects on growth hormone and IGF-1, raise cortisol and appetite, and are sold on the black market. 8

Questions to ask your doctor

  • Would the appetite and cortisol effects work against my goals?
  • How would we watch my blood sugar and IGF-1?
  • Is there an approved treatment for my actual goal or diagnosis?

Where it stands in the USsafety agent

FDA safety-risk list Not approved; on FDA's safety-risk list (503B). FDA lists GHRP-6 in Category 2 (significant safety risks) for outsourcing facilities and places it in Category 3 (nominated without adequate support) for ordinary compounding pharmacies, which leaves compounding open to FDA enforcement. WADA bans it in sport. 12345
