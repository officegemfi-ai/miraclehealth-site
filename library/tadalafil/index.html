What it is

Tadalafil is a phosphodiesterase type 5 (PDE5) inhibitor. During sexual stimulation, nerves and blood vessel linings release nitric oxide, which raises cGMP and relaxes smooth muscle so blood flows into the penis; blocking PDE5 keeps cGMP higher, so tadalafil has no effect without sexual stimulation. The same drug is sold as Adcirca for pulmonary arterial hypertension, and the two should not be taken together.

Strong evidence

FDA-approved for erectile dysfunction and for urinary symptoms of benign prostatic hyperplasia based on randomized trials; AUA guidelines recommend PDE5 inhibitors for ED and list tadalafil as an option for these urinary symptoms.

What it may do

  • Strong evidence

    Improved erections in men with erectile dysfunction: in two US trials, successful intercourse attempts rose to 50% and 64% versus 25% and 23% on placebo, with effects lasting up to 36 hours 1

  • Moderate evidence

    Improved erectile function compared with placebo in 216 men with type 1 or type 2 diabetes 1

  • Strong evidence

    Treats urinary symptoms of benign prostatic hyperplasia, with or without erectile dysfunction; the AUA lists it as a treatment option 12

  • Limited evidence

    In men with ED and testosterone deficiency confirmed by a clinician, the AUA says treating the deficiency may improve results (low-certainty evidence) 3

Risks and side effects

  • Common

    Headache (about 11% to 15% versus 5% on placebo), indigestion, back pain, muscle aches, nasal congestion, flushing and limb pain 1

  • Not known

    Severe drop in blood pressure if combined with nitrates or riociguat; added blood pressure lowering with alpha-blockers, other blood pressure drugs and heavy drinking 13

  • Not known

    Erection lasting more than 4 hours (priapism), which needs emergency care 1

  • Rare

    Sudden vision loss from NAION (damage to the optic nerve) and sudden hearing loss have been reported rarely; the label says to stop it and get medical care right away if either happens, and people who have had NAION before are at higher risk of it happening again 1

  • Not known

    Serious skin reactions, including Stevens-Johnson syndrome and exfoliative dermatitis, have been reported 1

Who should avoid it or check with a clinician first

  • Taking nitrates in any form, regularly or now and then (contraindicated)
  • Taking riociguat or another guanylate cyclase stimulator (contraindicated)
  • Heart conditions that make sexual activity unsafe, such as unstable angina or uncontrolled arrhythmia or blood pressure
  • Severe liver impairment
  • Past serious allergic reaction to tadalafil

Interactions

  • Nitrates: contraindicated; tadalafil lasts longer than other ED drugs, so if a nitrate is ever needed in an emergency, medical staff need to know when it was last taken
  • Alpha-blockers (such as doxazosin, tamsulosin or alfuzosin) and other blood pressure drugs: extra blood pressure lowering; for BPH, combining with an alpha-blocker is not recommended
  • Strong CYP3A4 inhibitors (ketoconazole, ritonavir, itraconazole; grapefruit juice likely): raise tadalafil levels
  • CYP3A4 inducers such as rifampin: sharply lower tadalafil levels
  • Other PDE5 inhibitors, including Adcirca: not to be combined

Blood markers to watch

Before starting

  • Morning total testosterone if low testosterone is suspected

While taking it

None routinely needed.

The label sets no routine lab tests; the AUA notes that erectile dysfunction can be a marker of underlying heart disease and that PDE5 inhibitors may work better when low testosterone is treated.

Key studies

  1. Guideline2018Men with erectile dysfunction

    Erectile Dysfunction: AUA Guideline

    Recommends telling men with ED about PDE5 inhibitors, finds sildenafil, tadalafil, vardenafil and avanafil similarly effective, and says men taking nitrates regularly should not use them. 3

  2. Guideline2021Men with urinary symptoms from an enlarged prostate

    Management of Lower Urinary Tract Symptoms Attributed to Benign Prostatic Hyperplasia: AUA Guideline

    Tadalafil should be discussed as an option whether or not a man has erectile dysfunction; combining it with an alpha-blocker adds no symptom benefit over either drug alone. 2

  3. Label2026Men with erectile dysfunction in two US trials, and 216 men with diabetes

    CIALIS (tadalafil) tablets: Prescribing Information

    Tadalafil raised the share of successful intercourse attempts versus placebo, with effects up to 36 hours after taking it. 1

Questions for your clinician

  • Is my heart healthy enough for sexual activity, and could my ED be an early sign of heart disease?
  • Do any of my medicines, including nitrates, alpha-blockers or other blood pressure drugs, interact with tadalafil?
  • Should my testosterone be checked?

Status in the US

FDA-approved FDA-approved for ED and enlarged-prostate symptoms. Approved for erectile dysfunction, the signs and symptoms of benign prostatic hyperplasia (BPH), and both together. Tadalafil is also sold as Adcirca for pulmonary arterial hypertension. 1
