What it is

SLU-PP-332 is a synthetic experimental compound developed by academic researchers as an 'exercise mimetic'. It activates all three estrogen-related receptors (ERR alpha, beta and gamma), proteins that control genes linked to exercise capacity and energy use, with the strongest effect on ERR alpha. In mice it raised endurance and energy use and reduced fat gain. It is not approved anywhere, and anti-doping scientists consider it relevant to sports drug testing.

Animal and lab studies

All published evidence comes from mice and lab cells: it raised endurance and reduced fat gain in mice, and no human studies have been published.

What it may do

  • Animal and lab studies

    Longer exercise endurance and more oxidative (type IIa) muscle fibers in mice 4

  • Animal and lab studies

    Less fat gain, better insulin sensitivity and higher energy use in obese mice; in one month, obese mice lost 12% of their body weight 51

  • Animal and lab studies

    More mitochondrial function and cell respiration in muscle cells in the lab 4

Risks and side effects

  • Not known

    No human safety data: no human studies have been published 1

  • Not known

    Animal safety is barely reported: its developers planned more animal testing for side effects before any human trials 1

  • Not known

    Unknown product quality: it is not an approved drug, so any product sold for personal use has not been reviewed by FDA; FDA has warned that drugs sold online labeled 'for research purposes' (in its case, GLP-1 drugs) are of unknown quality and may contain the wrong ingredient or amount 62

Who should avoid it or check with a clinician first

  • Everyone outside a research study: it has never been tested in people
  • Pregnant or breastfeeding (never studied)
  • Anyone with a chronic illness or on regular medicines (no human data on safety or interactions)
  • Athletes in tested sports: it is not named on WADA's 2026 list, but WADA's S0 class covers substances not approved for human use, and anti-doping labs are studying how to detect it

Interactions

  • None well documented (it has not been studied in people)

While taking it

None routinely needed.

SLU-PP-332 has not been studied in people, so no evidence-based monitoring exists.

Key studies

  1. Animal2024Diet-induced obese mice and genetically obese (ob/ob) mice

    A Synthetic ERR Agonist Alleviates Metabolic Syndrome

    Raised energy expenditure and fat burning, reduced fat mass and obesity, and improved insulin sensitivity. 5

  2. Animal2023Mice, plus a muscle cell line

    Synthetic ERRα/β/γ Agonist Induces an ERRα-Dependent Acute Aerobic Exercise Response and Enhances Exercise Capacity

    Activated all three ERRs (most strongly ERR alpha), increased oxidative muscle fibers and exercise endurance, and switched on an exercise-like gene program that depended on ERR alpha. 4

  3. Cell2026Human liver enzyme preparations in the lab

    In Vitro Metabolism and Analytical Characterization of SLU-PP-332 and SLU-PP-915: Novel Pan-ERR Agonists With Doping Potential

    Anti-doping scientists identified its breakdown products to support possible detection in sports drug testing (as summarized by the authors' institute). 2
