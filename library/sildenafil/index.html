What it is

Sildenafil is a phosphodiesterase type 5 (PDE5) inhibitor, the same drug class as tadalafil, which improves erections by strengthening the blood-flow signal released during sexual stimulation. As Viagra, it was tested in 21 placebo-controlled trials in more than 3,000 men aged 19 to 87 with erectile dysfunction. Viagra is not indicated for women or children.

Strong evidence

FDA-approved for erectile dysfunction after 21 randomized trials in more than 3,000 men; the AUA recommends telling men with ED about PDE5 inhibitors and finds the drugs in the class similarly effective.

What it may do

  • Strong evidence

    Improved erections in men aged 19 to 87 with erectile dysfunction of many causes: in pooled fixed-dose studies, 63% to 82% reported better erections versus 24% on placebo 1

  • Strong evidence

    Beat placebo in all 21 randomized trials in its approval program (more than 3,000 men) 1

  • Limited evidence

    In men with ED and low testosterone, PDE5 inhibitors may work better when combined with testosterone therapy (AUA; low-certainty evidence) 2

Risks and side effects

  • Common

    Headache (16% versus 4% on placebo), flushing (10% versus 1%), indigestion, nasal congestion, back and muscle pain, nausea, dizziness and rash 1

  • Uncommon

    Vision changes, mainly a color tinge or blurring 1

  • Not known

    Severe drop in blood pressure with nitrates or riociguat; added blood pressure lowering with alpha-blockers and other blood pressure drugs 12

  • Rare

    Sudden vision loss from NAION (damage to the optic nerve), reported rarely, and sudden hearing loss; a causal link has not been established 1

  • Not known

    Erection lasting more than 4 hours (priapism), which can cause permanent damage without prompt care 1

  • Not known

    Serious heart events, seizures, nosebleeds and blood in the urine reported after approval 1

Talk to a clinician first if you are

  • Taking nitrates or other nitric oxide donors in any form
  • Taking riociguat or another guanylate cyclase stimulator
  • Recent heart attack, stroke or life-threatening arrhythmia, or other serious heart disease, unless a clinician confirms sexual activity is safe
  • Already taking Revatio or another PDE5 inhibitor
  • Past allergic reaction to sildenafil

Interactions

  • Nitrates and other nitric oxide donors: contraindicated because of a dangerous drop in blood pressure
  • Alpha-blockers: added blood pressure lowering; the label advises that patients be stable on the alpha-blocker first
  • Ritonavir: raises sildenafil levels about 11-fold; erythromycin and saquinavir also raise levels substantially
  • Amlodipine and other blood pressure drugs: added blood pressure lowering

Blood markers to watch

Before starting

  • Morning total testosterone if low testosterone is suspected

While taking it

None routinely needed.

The label sets no routine lab tests; the AUA notes that erectile dysfunction can be a marker of underlying heart disease and that PDE5 inhibitors may work better when low testosterone is treated.

Key studies

  1. Guideline2018Men with erectile dysfunction

    Erectile Dysfunction: AUA Guideline

    Recommends telling men with ED about PDE5 inhibitors, finds sildenafil, tadalafil, vardenafil and avanafil similarly effective, and says men taking nitrates regularly should not use them. 2

  2. Label2017More than 3,000 men aged 19 to 87 with erectile dysfunction in 21 placebo-controlled trials

    VIAGRA (sildenafil citrate) tablets: Prescribing Information

    Sildenafil improved erections versus placebo in all 21 trials; headache and flushing were the most common side effects. 1

Questions for your clinician

  • Is my heart healthy enough for sexual activity, and could my ED be an early sign of heart disease?
  • Do any of my medicines, including nitrates, alpha-blockers or HIV drugs, interact with sildenafil?
  • Should my testosterone be checked?

Status in the US

FDA-approved FDA-approved for erectile dysfunction. Viagra is approved for erectile dysfunction in men and is not indicated for women or children. Sildenafil is also sold as Revatio for pulmonary arterial hypertension, and the Viagra label says not to combine the two. 1
