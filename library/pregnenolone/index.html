What it is

Pregnenolone is a neurosteroid, a steroid made in the body that is converted into other steroid hormones and into brain-active steroids such as allopregnanolone. In the US it is sold over the counter as a dietary supplement. Small trials have tested it as an add-on treatment for schizophrenia, bipolar depression and chronic low back pain.

Limited evidence

A handful of small randomized trials, each with about 100 people or fewer and lasting 4 to 12 weeks, show mixed benefits for back pain, schizophrenia symptoms and bipolar depression.

What it may do

  • Moderate evidence

    Less low back pain, and less pain interference with work and activity, than placebo over 4 weeks in a trial of 94 US veterans 5

  • Limited evidence

    Fewer negative symptoms (blunted emotion, low motivation, loss of pleasure) when added to antipsychotics in an 8-week trial of 60 people with recent-onset schizophrenia 6

  • Limited evidence

    Better depression scores over time on a clinician-rated scale, and higher remission on a self-report scale (61% versus 37%), when added to usual treatment in 80 adults with bipolar depression; the two scales did not agree 7

  • Limited evidence

    Better attention, working memory or verbal memory in small pilot trials in schizophrenia 8

Risks and side effects

  • Not known

    Mild restlessness, cold hands and feet, muscle pain or stiffness and headache, reported more often than with placebo in earlier trials 1

  • In theory

    Can raise levels of other steroids made from it, so trials excluded people with prostate, uterine or breast cancer 17

  • Rare

    One published report of palpitations 1

  • Not known

    Long-term safety unknown: trials lasted 4 to 12 weeks 567

Who should avoid it or check with a clinician first

  • Prostate, uterine or breast cancer (excluded from trials because downstream hormones could rise)
  • Pregnant or breastfeeding (excluded from trials)

Interactions

  • Birth control pills and other hormone supplements: users were excluded from trials, so combined effects are unknown

Blood markers to watch

Before starting

None routinely needed.

While taking it

None routinely needed.

No evidence-based monitoring exists for supplement use; trials measured neurosteroid levels for research, and one planned a follow-up ECG because of a single palpitations report.

Key studies

  1. RCT202094 US veterans with chronic low back pain (4 weeks)

    Effect of Pregnenolone vs Placebo on Self-reported Chronic Low Back Pain Among US Military Veterans: A Randomized Clinical Trial

    Pregnenolone lowered daily pain ratings and pain interference with work and activity more than placebo and was well tolerated. 5

  2. RCT201460 people with recent-onset schizophrenia or schizoaffective disorder (8 weeks)

    Pregnenolone treatment reduces severity of negative symptoms in recent-onset schizophrenia: an 8-week, double-blind, randomized add-on two-center trial

    Added to antipsychotics, pregnenolone reduced negative symptoms versus placebo, mainly in people not taking mood stabilizers; other symptoms and functioning did not change. 6

  3. RCT201480 adults with bipolar depression (12 weeks)

    A Randomized, Double-Blind, Placebo-Controlled Trial of Pregnenolone for Bipolar Depression

    Clinician-rated depression improved more over time with pregnenolone and self-rated remission was higher, but the two depression scales gave different results; it was well tolerated. 7

  4. Review2011Animal studies and pilot trials in schizophrenia

    Pregnenolone as a novel therapeutic candidate in schizophrenia: emerging preclinical and clinical evidence

    Early pilot trials suggested pregnenolone may reduce negative symptoms and improve some cognitive measures; the authors call for larger studies. 8

Questions for your clinician

  • Could pregnenolone raise my other hormone levels, given my health history?
  • Is there evidence for my specific condition, and how would we tell whether it is helping?
  • Does it interact with my birth control or other hormones?

Status in the US

Dietary supplement Supplement; FDA reviewing it for compounding. Pregnenolone is sold over the counter as a dietary supplement, which FDA does not approve for safety or effectiveness before sale. FDA also lists it in Category 1 (under evaluation) for pharmacy compounding, so pharmacies may compound it while FDA decides. 1234
