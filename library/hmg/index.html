What it is

Human menopausal gonadotropin (hMG, menotropins) is a mix of the pituitary hormones FSH and LH, purified from the urine of postmenopausal women; in Menopur, most of the LH activity comes from hCG. FSH and LH drive egg development in women and, in men, sperm and testosterone production in the testes. Menopur's US approval was converted to a biologics license on March 23, 2020.

Strong evidence

FDA-approved for multiple-egg development in IVF based on a randomized trial of 373 women; use in men is off-label, with only indirect evidence (a meta-analysis of 44 studies of gonadotropin therapy that did not report hMG separately).

What it may do

  • Strong evidence

    Supports multiple-egg development for IVF: in a trial of 373 women, the continuing pregnancy rate (23%) was not inferior to recombinant FSH 1

  • Limited evidence

    Gonadotropin therapy, including urine-derived products such as hMG, produced sperm in the ejaculate in about 75% of men with hypogonadotropic hypogonadism and no sperm, across 44 studies (off-label for hMG in the US) 3

  • Limited evidence

    In that meta-analysis of men with hypogonadotropic hypogonadism, earlier testosterone treatment did not reduce the success of gonadotropin therapy, and results were better when hypogonadism began after puberty; this says nothing about recovery after testosterone or steroid use in other men 3

Risks and side effects

  • Uncommon

    Ovarian hyperstimulation syndrome in 7.2% of women in the IVF trial; it can be serious and can start after treatment stops 1

  • Common

    Multiple pregnancy: 35% of pregnancies in the IVF trial were multiples 1

  • Not known

    Blood clots and lung complications, with rare deaths 1

  • Not known

    Ovarian torsion, ectopic pregnancy and miscarriage 1

  • Uncommon

    Abdominal pain (6.7%), headache (6.2%) and injection-site pain or reactions (3.9%) 1

  • Not known

    Its US label covers only women; in men treated with FSH plus hCG, the Gonal-f label reports acne and breast enlargement 14

Who should avoid it or check with a clinician first

  • Pregnancy
  • High FSH showing primary ovarian failure
  • Uncontrolled thyroid, adrenal or pituitary disorders
  • Sex hormone-dependent tumors of the reproductive tract, or pituitary or hypothalamic tumors
  • Abnormal uterine bleeding, or ovarian cysts or enlargement of unknown cause not due to PCOS

Interactions

  • None well documented

Blood markers to watch

Before starting

  • Pregnancy test
  • FSH (to rule out primary ovarian failure)
  • Evaluation of the male partner's fertility
  • Men (off-label): testosterone, LH, FSH and semen analysis

While taking it

  • Ultrasound and estradiol levels to track ovarian response
  • Men (off-label): semen analysis and testosterone

The label calls for evaluating the male partner and monitoring ovarian response with ultrasound and estradiol; for off-label use in men, studies tracked sperm in the ejaculate.

Key studies

  1. Meta-analysis2014Men with hypogonadotropic hypogonadism and no sperm (44 gonadotropin studies and 16 GnRH studies)

    Factors affecting spermatogenesis upon gonadotropin-replacement therapy: a meta-analytic study

    Gonadotropin therapy produced sperm in about 75% of men; success was higher with hCG plus FSH and with onset after puberty, and earlier testosterone therapy did not affect results in these men. 3

  2. Label2025373 women undergoing IVF or IVF with ICSI

    MENOPUR (menotropins for injection): Prescribing Information

    Continuing pregnancy (23%) was non-inferior to recombinant FSH; ovarian hyperstimulation occurred in 7.2%, and 35% of pregnancies were multiples. 1

Questions for your clinician

  • Is my infertility caused by low pituitary hormones, so that gonadotropins could help?
  • How will we watch for ovarian hyperstimulation and multiple pregnancy?
  • For men: how long does it usually take to see sperm, and how will we track progress?

Status in the US

FDA-approved FDA-approved biologic for IVF; off-label in men. Menopur is approved for developing multiple follicles and pregnancy in ovulatory women undergoing assisted reproduction such as IVF; its label has no use in men. Its approval was converted to a biologics license on March 23, 2020. For athletes: WADA bans hCG and LH in males, and hMG has LH activity. 125
