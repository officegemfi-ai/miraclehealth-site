What it isevidence librarian

Human chorionic gonadotropin (hCG) is a protein hormone made by the placenta during pregnancy. It acts much like luteinizing hormone (LH), the pituitary hormone that signals the testes and ovaries, with a small amount of FSH-like activity. Products such as Pregnyl are purified from the urine of pregnant women and given by injection. Its label states it has not been shown to help treat obesity.

Strong evidence

It is an FDA-approved prescription drug for male hypogonadism of pituitary origin, undescended testes and ovulation induction. The evidence for its off-label use alongside testosterone comes from small, short studies.

What it may doevidence librarian

  • Strong evidence

    Treats hypogonadism caused by a pituitary deficiency in selected men 1

  • Strong evidence

    After menotropins, induces ovulation in some infertile women 1

  • Limited evidence

    Approved for undescended testes in boys when there is no physical blockage, but the American Urological Association's 2025 guideline says hormonal therapy should not be used to bring the testes down, citing low response rates 15

  • Limited evidence

    Kept testosterone inside the testes near normal in 29 healthy men whose own LH and FSH were shut down by testosterone for 3 weeks; testosterone alone cut it by 94% (studied for off-label use alongside testosterone therapy) 4

Risks and side effectssafety agent

  • Not known

    Ovarian hyperstimulation syndrome (sudden ovarian enlargement with fluid in the abdomen or chest) when used to induce ovulation 1

  • Not known

    Multiple pregnancy (twins or more) with ovulation induction 1

  • Not known

    Breast enlargement in men, fluid retention, headache, irritability, restlessness, depression and fatigue 1

  • Not known

    Early puberty in boys treated for undescended testes 1

  • Not known

    Blood clots in arteries, and allergic reactions including anaphylaxis 1

  • Not known

    Very low-calorie 'hCG diets' can cause gallstones, electrolyte imbalance and irregular heartbeat; FDA says any weight loss comes from the diet, not the hCG 3

Who should skip it, or ask a doctor first

  • Early (precocious) puberty
  • Prostate cancer or another androgen-dependent tumor
  • Past allergic reaction to hCG
  • Heart or kidney disease, epilepsy, migraine or asthma (needs caution because of fluid retention)

What it can clash with

  • None well documented

Blood tests to watchmarkers agent

Before starting

  • Morning testosterone with LH and FSH, to confirm the cause of low testosterone
  • Semen analysis if fertility is the goal

While taking it

  • Testosterone levels
  • Semen analysis if fertility is the goal

The hCG label sets no lab schedule for men; tests like these show whether the testes are responding. Ovulation induction is managed by clinicians experienced in infertility, as the label requires.

The main studiesevidence librarian

  1. RCT200529 healthy men

    Low-Dose Human Chorionic Gonadotropin Maintains Intratesticular Testosterone in Normal Men with Testosterone-Induced Gonadotropin Suppression

    Testosterone suppressed LH and FSH and cut testosterone inside the testes by 94% over 3 weeks; adding hCG limited the drop to 25% or less, or raised the level, depending on the amount. Sperm production was not measured. 4

  2. Label2011Boys with undescended testes, men with pituitary-related hypogonadism and women undergoing ovulation induction

    PREGNYL (chorionic gonadotropin for injection, USP): Prescribing Information

    Lists the approved uses and states that hCG has not been demonstrated to be effective additional therapy for obesity. 1

Questions to ask your doctor

  • Is my low testosterone caused by a pituitary problem that hCG could treat?
  • If I need testosterone therapy, how can we protect my fertility?
  • Which symptoms, such as breast swelling or fluid retention, should I report?

Where it stands in the USsafety agent

FDA-approved FDA-approved prescription biologic; not for weight loss. Approved (for example as Pregnyl) for undescended testes, hypogonadism of pituitary origin in men and, after menotropins, ovulation induction. It has been regulated as a biologic since March 2020, which the compounding pharmacists' trade group says put it outside the reach of most compounding pharmacies; FDA has said over-the-counter and 'homeopathic' hCG diet products are illegal. 123
