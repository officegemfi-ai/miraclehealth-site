Also called HGH, GH, Growth hormone, rHGH, Genotropin, Norditropin, Omnitrope, Humatrope, Saizen, Serostim, Zomacton

What it isevidence librarian

Somatropin is recombinant human growth hormone: a protein of 191 amino acids made in E. coli bacteria, with the same sequence as the growth hormone made by the pituitary gland. In children it promotes growth. In adults it shifts body composition toward more lean mass and less fat. Blood levels of IGF-1 rise during treatment. It is given by injection.

Strong evidence

It is FDA-approved for growth hormone deficiency and several childhood growth disorders. In healthy older adults, a review of 18 small trials found small changes in body composition and more side effects.

What it may doevidence librarian

  • Strong evidence

    Improves growth in children with growth hormone deficiency, Prader-Willi syndrome, Turner syndrome, small-for-gestational-age short stature or idiopathic short stature 1

  • Strong evidence

    More lean mass and less body fat and waist size than placebo after 6 months in adults with growth hormone deficiency, maintained with continued treatment 1

  • Moderate evidence

    About 2 kg more lean mass and 2 kg less fat, with no change in weight or bone density, in healthy older adults (18 small trials, 220 treated, average age 69); not an approved use, distributing it for anti-aging is illegal in the US, and the reviewers concluded it cannot be recommended 234

Risks and side effectssafety agent

  • Common

    Swelling of the hands and feet, joint pain, limb pain, tingling and stiffness: swelling in 17.5% versus 5.1% on placebo in adults 1

  • Uncommon

    Carpal tunnel syndrome (about 2% in adult extension studies) and, in healthy older adults, breast enlargement in men 13

  • Not known

    Higher blood sugar: it can unmask impaired glucose tolerance or diabetes, and new diabetes or impaired fasting glucose was somewhat more likely in healthy older adults 13

  • Not known

    Raised pressure inside the skull (intracranial hypertension), which usually resolves after stopping or lowering the dose 1

  • Not known

    Growth of existing tumors, and second tumors (especially meningiomas) in childhood cancer survivors treated with head radiation 1

  • Not known

    Higher death rates when given to critically ill adults after major surgery, trauma or acute respiratory failure 1

  • Not known

    In children: slipped growth plate at the hip (a new limp or hip or knee pain needs checking), and faster worsening of an existing scoliosis during rapid growth 1

  • Rare

    Pancreatitis, to be considered with persistent severe abdominal pain, especially in children (girls with Turner syndrome may be at greater risk) 1

Who should skip it, or ask a doctor first

  • Acute critical illness, such as after open-heart or abdominal surgery, major trauma or acute respiratory failure
  • Active cancer
  • Active proliferative or severe nonproliferative diabetic retinopathy
  • Prader-Willi syndrome with severe obesity, airway obstruction, sleep apnea or severe breathing problems
  • Past serious allergic reaction to somatropin

What it can clash with

  • Glucocorticoids: replacement doses may need adjusting, and they can blunt growth in children
  • Insulin and other diabetes medicines: doses may need adjusting
  • Oral estrogen: can reduce its effect
  • Drugs cleared by CYP450 liver enzymes, such as sex steroids, anticonvulsants and cyclosporine: levels may change

Blood tests to watchmarkers agent

Before starting

  • IGF-1
  • Fasting glucose or HbA1c
  • Thyroid function
  • Eye exam (fundoscopy)
  • Cortisol if adrenal insufficiency is possible

While taking it

  • IGF-1
  • Glucose periodically
  • Thyroid function periodically
  • Eye exams periodically

The label calls for periodic glucose and thyroid checks and eye exams for raised pressure in the skull; IGF-1, phosphate, alkaline phosphatase and parathyroid hormone may rise during treatment.

The main studiesevidence librarian

  1. Systematic review2007220 healthy older adults given growth hormone in 18 randomized study populations (average age 69)

    Systematic Review: The Safety and Efficacy of Growth Hormone in the Healthy Elderly

    Lean mass rose about 2.1 kg and fat fell about 2.1 kg with no change in weight or bone density; swelling, joint pain, carpal tunnel syndrome and breast enlargement were more common. The authors concluded growth hormone cannot be recommended as an anti-aging therapy. 3

  2. Label2025Adults with growth hormone deficiency (placebo-controlled trials)

    GENOTROPIN (somatropin) for injection: Prescribing Information

    At 6 months, lean body mass rose and body fat and waist size fell versus placebo; bone density dipped at 6 months and returned to baseline by 12 months. Swelling and joint pain were the most common side effects. 1

Questions to ask your doctor

  • Have I been properly tested for growth hormone deficiency, and what did the tests show?
  • How will we watch my blood sugar, thyroid and eyes during treatment?
  • What are the safety and legal concerns with growth hormone used outside an approved condition?

Where it stands in the USsafety agent

FDA-approved FDA-approved; illegal to distribute for anti-aging. Approved as a prescription biologic for children with growth hormone deficiency, Prader-Willi syndrome, Turner syndrome, short stature after being born small for gestational age or idiopathic short stature, and for adults with growth hormone deficiency. Federal law (21 U.S.C. 333(e)) makes distributing it for other uses a crime punishable by up to 5 years in prison, FDA says no anti-aging, bodybuilding or athletic use has been authorized, and as a biologic it cannot be compounded under sections 503A or 503B. 12
