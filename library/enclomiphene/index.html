Enclomiphene

Also called Androxal, Enclomiphene citrate

One of the two forms in the fertility drug clomiphene, studied as a pill to raise men's own testosterone; it is not approved in the US or Europe.

What it is

Enclomiphene is the trans isomer of clomiphene; clomiphene tablets are a mix of enclomiphene and zuclomiphene. It is a selective estrogen receptor modulator (SERM) that acts as an estrogen blocker, and in men it raises the pituitary hormones LH and FSH, which signal the testes to make testosterone and sperm. A drug company sought US approval to treat secondary hypogonadism (low testosterone because the pituitary sends too little LH), but FDA declined to approve it in 2015 (a complete response letter), and the European Medicines Agency refused approval in 2018.

Moderate evidence

Several randomized trials, the largest in 256 men, show it raises testosterone, LH and FSH, but FDA concluded the trials did not show a clinically meaningful improvement in symptoms or signs of low testosterone.

What it may do

  • Moderate evidence

    Raised testosterone, LH and FSH compared with placebo in randomized trials of men with secondary hypogonadism, but FDA found no clinically meaningful improvement in symptoms or signs 54

  • Limited evidence

    Kept sperm concentration higher than testosterone gel did, at similar testosterone levels (trials pooled with clomiphene) 5

Risks and side effects

  • Not known

    Blood clots: Europe's regulator cited a risk of venous thromboembolism, and FDA listed pulmonary embolism, deep vein thrombosis and other heart and clotting events among serious adverse events in the trials 64

  • Not known

    Rises in estradiol, PSA and hematocrit (the share of red blood cells); a few trial participants stopped because of high PSA or hematocrit 45

  • Not known

    Long-term safety is unknown: FDA noted long-term data are lacking and that drug-level data in liver or kidney impairment are incomplete 4

  • In theory

    Possible eye effects: clomiphene, which contains enclomiphene, can cause visual symptoms, and cataracts were seen in animal studies of enclomiphene 74

  • Not known

    Headache and other mostly mild side effects; in the largest trial, side effects were not more frequent with enclomiphene than with placebo, but the trials were too small to rule out rare serious events such as blood clots 5

Who should avoid it or check with a clinician first

  • History of blood clots (deep vein thrombosis or pulmonary embolism)
  • Low testosterone caused by testicular failure (primary hypogonadism); it was studied only for secondary hypogonadism
  • Liver or kidney disease (not well studied)
  • Athletes tested under anti-doping rules: WADA bans SERMs such as clomiphene, and its list is not exhaustive

Interactions

  • None well documented

Blood markers to watch

Before starting

  • Morning total testosterone, LH and FSH
  • Estradiol
  • Hematocrit
  • PSA
  • Semen analysis if fertility is a goal

While taking it

  • Testosterone and estradiol
  • Hematocrit
  • PSA

There is no approved label; these follow what the trials measured and the safety signals FDA flagged (rises in estradiol, PSA and hematocrit).

Key studies

  1. Meta-analysis2025819 men with hypogonadism in 10 randomized trials, 4 of them testing enclomiphene

    Clomiphene or enclomiphene citrate for the treatment of male hypogonadism: a systematic review and meta-analysis of randomized controlled trials

    Clomiphene or enclomiphene raised testosterone, LH and FSH versus placebo and reached testosterone levels similar to testosterone gel with higher sperm concentration; results were pooled, so enclomiphene alone was not estimated. 5

  2. Review2022FDA's review of enclomiphene trials in men with secondary hypogonadism

    Pharmacy Compounding Advisory Committee meeting, June 8, 2022: transcript

    FDA said the trials raised hormone levels but did not show clinically meaningful improvement in symptoms or signs, flagged heart, clotting, estradiol, PSA and hematocrit concerns, and proposed not allowing compounding with it. 4

  3. Review2018Overweight men with hypogonadotropic hypogonadism (company application to the EU)

    EnCyzix (enclomifene): refusal of marketing authorisation

    Testosterone rose, but the studies did not look at symptoms such as bone strength, weight gain, impotence and libido; with a risk of venous thromboembolism, the regulator judged that benefits did not outweigh risks. 6

Questions for your clinician

  • Is my low testosterone secondary (from low LH), and how does this unapproved option compare with approved treatments?
  • If I want to keep my fertility, which options best protect sperm production?
  • Which blood tests, such as estradiol, hematocrit and PSA, should we check, and how often?

Status in the US

Compounded by prescription Not FDA-approved; FDA advisers voted against compounding. No enclomiphene product is FDA-approved; FDA declined the maker's application in 2015. FDA lists enclomiphene citrate in Category 1 (under evaluation), so under its interim policy pharmacies may compound it for now. But FDA recommended in 2022 against adding it to the list of allowed bulk substances, its advisory committee agreed 8 to 4, and this could change whenever FDA makes a final decision. 1234
