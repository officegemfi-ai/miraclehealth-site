Medication · Cognition & focus

Modafinil.

Also called Provigil

A prescription drug that keeps people awake, for narcolepsy, sleep apnea and shift-work sleepiness. Also taken for focus, which it isn't approved for. In rested, healthy people its effects are small.

What it isevidence librarian

Modafinil, sold as Provigil, is a prescription drug that helps people stay awake. It is approved for adults with excessive sleepiness from narcolepsy, obstructive sleep apnea or shift work disorder. How it works is not known. It is a Schedule IV controlled substance, and prescribers are told to watch for misuse.

Moderate evidence

It is approved for sleepiness from three sleep disorders. For focus in rested, healthy people, meta-analyses of small placebo-controlled trials found only small effects, on a few kinds of thinking tests.

What it may doevidence librarian

  • Strong evidence

    Improves wakefulness in adults with excessive sleepiness from narcolepsy, obstructive sleep apnea or shift work disorder (approved use) 1

  • Moderate evidence

    A small overall gain on thinking tests in adults who were not sleep-deprived (average effect size 0.10 across 19 placebo-controlled trials with 767 people), mainly in executive function and processing speed; attention and memory did not improve 2

  • Moderate evidence

    A separate set of meta-analyses of 14 studies in healthy rested adults, using different methods, found a clear gain only in memory updating (a working-memory task), and the authors said the evidence does not support users' belief that it is an effective cognitive enhancer 3

Risks and side effectssafety agent

  • Common

    Headache, the most common side effect, in about a third of patients versus about a quarter on placebo; nausea, nervousness, anxiety, insomnia and dizziness are among the other frequent ones 1

  • Rare

    Serious skin reactions, including Stevens-Johnson syndrome and toxic epidermal necrolysis, are rare but can be life-threatening; any new rash needs prompt medical review 1

  • Not known

    Allergic reactions: swelling of the face and throat (angioedema), anaphylaxis and multi-organ reactions, including at least one death reported after approval 1

  • Not known

    Psychiatric symptoms; the label urges caution in people with a history of psychosis, depression or mania 1

  • Not known

    Heart concerns: not recommended for people with a history of left ventricular hypertrophy (thickened heart muscle) or with mitral valve prolapse that caused symptoms on stimulants; the label says closer monitoring of heart rate and blood pressure may be appropriate 1

  • Not known

    Misuse and dependence: a Schedule IV controlled substance, and prescribers are told to watch for misuse, especially in people with a history of drug or stimulant misuse 1

Who should skip it, or ask a doctor first

  • Pregnant or planning pregnancy (animal data suggest harm, and fetal growth restriction and miscarriage have been reported)
  • Allergy to modafinil or armodafinil
  • History of left ventricular hypertrophy or other heart disease (needs a doctor first)
  • History of psychosis, depression or mania (needs a doctor first)
  • Athletes in tested sports (WADA bans modafinil in competition)

What it can clash with

  • Hormonal birth control: may fail during use and for one month after stopping; the label advises another or an additional method
  • Cyclosporine: blood levels may drop
  • Omeprazole, phenytoin, diazepam, propranolol, clomipramine and other drugs cleared by the CYP2C19 enzyme: levels may rise
  • Warfarin: more frequent INR checks may be needed
  • MAO inhibitors: use with caution

The main studiesevidence librarian

  1. Meta-analysis201919 placebo-controlled trials, 767 adults who were not sleep-deprived

    The Efficacy of Modafinil as a Cognitive Enhancer: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis

    The overall effect on cognition was small but significant (g = 0.10), driven by executive function and processing speed; the authors concluded it has only limited potential as a cognitive enhancer in rested people. 2

  2. Meta-analysis202014 modafinil studies in healthy, non-sleep-deprived adults (plus studies of two other stimulants)

    How effective are pharmaceuticals for cognitive enhancement in healthy adults? A series of meta-analyses of cognitive performance during acute administration of modafinil, methylphenidate and D-amphetamine

    Modafinil had a small overall effect (SMD 0.12), mainly on memory updating; the authors said the effects are small and the test conditions may not reflect real-world use. 3

  3. Label2025Adults with narcolepsy, obstructive sleep apnea or shift work disorder

    PROVIGIL (modafinil) tablets: prescribing information

    Approved to improve wakefulness in these conditions; warns of serious rash and allergic and psychiatric reactions, and of reduced effectiveness of hormonal birth control. 1

Questions to ask your doctor

  • Is my daytime sleepiness from a sleep disorder that should be diagnosed and treated first?
  • Will modafinil affect my birth control or my other medicines?
  • Given my heart and mental-health history, is it safe for me?

Where it stands in the USsafety agent

FDA-approved FDA-approved for sleep disorders; Schedule IV. Provigil is approved to improve wakefulness in adults with excessive sleepiness from narcolepsy, obstructive sleep apnea (it treats the sleepiness, not the airway obstruction) or shift work disorder. It is a Schedule IV controlled substance, it is not approved for children, and use for focus in healthy people is off-label. 1
