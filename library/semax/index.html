What it isevidence librarian

Semax is a lab-made chain of seven amino acids, modeled on a fragment of ACTH, a pituitary hormone. It is a registered drug in Russia, sold there as nasal drops. It is not approved in the US. In rats it raises brain levels of growth factors such as BDNF, which support nerve cells. FDA reviewers note that how it works is poorly understood.

Limited evidence

Rat studies show effects on brain growth factors. But the human data are a few small Russian studies, and FDA judged the evidence insufficient for stroke, migraine and nerve pain.

What it may doevidence librarian

  • Animal and lab studies

    Raised BDNF and its receptor in the hippocampus and improved avoidance learning in rats 7

  • Animal and lab studies

    Countered the drop in brain growth-factor genes after reduced blood flow to the brain in rats 8

  • Limited evidence

    Higher blood BDNF and faster functional gains in a Russian study of 110 adults in stroke rehabilitation; whether the study was randomized isn't clear 9

Risks and side effectssafety agent

  • In theory

    Possible immune reactions to impurities or clumped peptide in unregulated products 4

  • In theory

    Boosted amphetamine-triggered dopamine release in mice; abuse potential has not been studied 4

  • In theory

    Anti-clotting effects in animal studies; bleeding risk in people is unclear 4

  • Not known

    No standard toxicity studies, including reproductive safety, were found by FDA 4

  • Not known

    One FDA adverse event report of eye pain and burning after using nasal drops bought online 4

Who should skip it, or ask a doctor first

  • Pregnant or breastfeeding (no reproductive safety data)
  • Bleeding disorder or taking blood thinners
  • Taking stimulant medicines, or a history of stimulant misuse

What it can clash with

  • Amphetamine and other stimulants (semax increased amphetamine-induced dopamine release in mice; not studied in people)
  • Blood thinners and antiplatelet drugs (anti-clotting effects seen in animals; not studied in people)

The main studiesevidence librarian

  1. Trial2018110 adults in rehabilitation after ischemic stroke (Russia)

    The efficacy of semax in the tretament [sic] of patients at different stages of ischemic stroke

    People given semax had higher blood BDNF and faster functional improvement than those who were not. The abstract does not say whether patients were randomized or blinded. 9

  2. Review2026Published studies in about 33 to 47 healthy adults, 69 adults with medical conditions and 451 children

    Semax – Related Bulk Drug Substances (Semax (free base) and Semax acetate)

    FDA found insufficient evidence of effectiveness for cerebral ischemia, migraine or trigeminal neuralgia, little safety data, and possible immunogenicity and abuse-potential concerns. 4

  3. Animal2006Rats

    Semax, an analog of ACTH(4–10) with cognitive effects, regulates BDNF and trkB expression in the rat hippocampus

    A single treatment raised hippocampal BDNF protein about 1.4-fold and trkB activation about 1.6-fold, with better performance on a learning task. 7

  4. Animal2011Rats

    Effect of semax and its C-terminal peptide PGP on expression of neurotrophins and their receptors in rat brain during incomplete global ischemia

    Semax counteracted the ischemia-induced fall in neurotrophin and receptor gene expression, most strongly in the hippocampus. 8

Questions to ask your doctor

  • Is there an approved treatment with stronger evidence for the problem I want semax to help with?
  • Could semax interact with any stimulant or blood-thinning medicine I take?
  • How could I know what is really in a product that FDA does not regulate?

Where it stands in the USsafety agent

Under FDA review Not FDA-approved; compounding decision pending. FDA dropped semax from its Category 2 safety-risk list in April 2026, which did not make it legal to compound, and in July 2026 its advisers voted 8-5 to add it to the compounding list, against FDA staff advice. As of mid-October 2026 no rule had been started, and an interim access policy for certain peptides, sent for White House review on October 14, had not been issued. 123456
