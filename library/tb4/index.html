What it isevidence librarian

Thymosin beta-4 is a protein of 43 amino acids, first isolated from calf thymus and found in blood, saliva, tears and wound fluid. Inside cells it binds actin, a building block of the cell skeleton. In several animal models it speeds skin repair. A drug company has tested it in people as eye drops (RGN-259) and as a skin gel (RGN-137). FDA stresses that TB-500, a short lab-made peptide based on one part of it, is not the same substance.

Limited evidence

The human evidence is mixed. The eye drops did not reach statistical significance in an 18-person cornea trial and missed their main goals in larger dry-eye and cornea trials. Wound trials of the skin gel gave inconsistent reports. The evidence for injury recovery comes from animal studies.

What it may doevidence librarian

  • Limited evidence

    In neurotrophic keratopathy, a rare nerve-related cornea disease, persistent corneal wounds healed by 4 weeks in 6 of 10 people on the eye drops versus 1 of 8 on placebo, not a statistically significant difference in a trial stopped early for slow recruitment; a later European phase 3 trial missed its main goal 312

  • Limited evidence

    In dry eye, a 72-person phase 2 trial and three phase 3 trials in more than 1,600 people missed their main goals; some symptoms, such as discomfort and grittiness, improved in secondary analyses 45

  • Limited evidence

    Skin-gel trials in 71 people with pressure ulcers and 72 with venous leg ulcers found it well tolerated; the developer reported no significant improvement in complete healing of pressure ulcers, while a later summary described faster healing 67

  • Limited evidence

    In 30 people with epidermolysis bullosa, a fragile, blistering skin disease, wounds tended to heal better than with placebo by day 15 7

  • Animal and lab studies

    Faster skin-wound repair in several animal models, including aged mice 84

Risks and side effectssafety agent

  • Not known

    Long-term safety is unknown: the eye-drop and skin-gel trials were short or small and reported no serious drug-related side effects, but there are almost no human safety data for injecting it 436

  • In theory

    Possible cancer concern: thymosin beta-4 drives new blood vessel growth and plays a role in tumor progression in research studies; its effect on existing cancers in people is unknown 9

  • In theory

    Immune reactions: FDA says injected peptides may pose a significant risk of immune reactions 8

  • Not known

    Mix-ups with TB-500: FDA says TB-500 is not the same substance, so findings for one do not apply to the other 8

  • Not known

    Unregulated products: FDA found that sellers of "research use only" peptides often treat TB-500 and thymosin beta-4 as interchangeable, and that purity, impurities and bacterial endotoxins are not controlled for TB-500, so a vial sold under either name may not contain what it says 8

Who should skip it, or ask a doctor first

  • Pregnant or breastfeeding (not studied)
  • Current or past cancer, unless a cancer specialist advises otherwise (linked to blood vessel growth and tumor progression in research)
  • Athletes subject to anti-doping rules (WADA bans thymosin beta-4 and its derivatives)

What it can clash with

  • None well documented

The main studiesevidence librarian

  1. RCT201572 adults with dry eye (28 days)

    Thymosin beta 4 ophthalmic solution for dry eye: a randomized, placebo-controlled, Phase II clinical trial conducted using the controlled adverse environment (CAE™) model

    The main goals, corneal staining and eye discomfort, were not met; some secondary measures of discomfort and staining improved, and no safety issues arose. 4

  2. RCT202318 people with neurotrophic keratopathy

    RGN-259 (Thymosin β4) Ophthalmic Solution Promotes Healing and Improves Comfort in Neurotrophic Keratopathy Patients in a Randomized, Placebo-Controlled, Double-Masked Phase III Clinical Trial

    Complete healing at 4 weeks in 6 of 10 on the drops versus 1 of 8 on placebo (p=0.07); healing differed significantly two weeks after treatment ended, comfort improved, and no significant adverse effects were seen. 3

  3. Review2026Published research on TB-500 and related peptides

    FDA Briefing Document for TB-500-Related Bulk Drug Substances (TB-500 (free base) and TB-500 acetate)

    FDA states that thymosin beta-4 and TB-500 are not the same substance and describes thymosin beta-4 as a 43-amino-acid, actin-binding peptide found in body fluids such as blood, saliva and tears. 8

  4. Review2007Lab and animal research

    Thymosin β4 and angiogenesis: modes of action and therapeutic potential

    Reviews how thymosin beta-4 regulates new blood vessel growth and its roles in wound healing, tumor progression and possible treatment of heart disease. 9

  • Which proven treatments should I try first for my injury or wound?
  • Given my personal and family cancer history, are there reasons to avoid it?

Where it stands in the USsafety agent

In clinical trials Not approved; eye drops in phase 3 trials. No thymosin beta-4 product is approved. Its eye-drop form, RGN-259, has FDA orphan drug designation for neurotrophic keratopathy; a European phase 3 trial missed its main goal in 2025, and results of a mostly US phase 3 trial were expected in November 2026. 12
