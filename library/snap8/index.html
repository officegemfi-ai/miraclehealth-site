What it is

SNAP-8 is a synthetic chain of eight amino acids (acetyl octapeptide-3) added to creams and serums. It copies the end of SNAP-25, a protein that nerve endings use to release signaling chemicals, and in lab tests it partly blocked the protein complex involved in that release. It is a slightly longer version of argireline (acetyl hexapeptide-3). Peptides in general cross the skin's outer layer poorly, which limits how much of a topical peptide gets through.

Limited evidence

Support comes from lab tests, manufacturer-reported wrinkle data and small studies of multi-ingredient products; no independent controlled trial of SNAP-8 alone was found.

What it may do

  • Limited evidence

    Shallower expression wrinkles in manufacturer testing (up to 62%, about 35% on average); these figures are not from peer-reviewed trials 1

  • Animal and lab studies

    Partly blocks the nerve-signal protein complex (SNARE) in lab tests, the basis for its Botox-like claim 1

  • Limited evidence

    Better hydration, dermal thickness and skin density in a small study of 20 women using microneedle patches that combined it with other active ingredients 4

  • Limited evidence

    Its shorter relative, argireline, reduced eye-area wrinkles compared with placebo in a 4-week trial of 60 Chinese adults 5

Risks and side effects

  • Not known

    Little independent safety testing; most published data on it come from the manufacturer 1

  • Not known

    Skin products that carry it are not reviewed by FDA for safety or effectiveness before sale 2

Who should avoid it or check with a clinician first

  • Pregnant or breastfeeding, since no safety data exist
  • Considering injecting it: it has only been studied in skin products

Interactions

  • None well documented

Blood markers to watch

Before starting

None routinely needed.

While taking it

None routinely needed.

No blood tests are needed for a topical cosmetic ingredient, and none have been studied for it.

Key studies

  1. RCT201360 Chinese adults with eye-area wrinkles

    The anti-wrinkle efficacy of argireline, a synthetic hexapeptide, in Chinese subjects: a randomized, placebo-controlled study

    The related peptide argireline, used for 4 weeks, reduced wrinkle roughness compared with placebo. SNAP-8 itself was not tested. 5

  2. Review2023Review; describes a study in 20 women over 41

    3D Printed Hollow Microneedles for Treating Skin Wrinkles Using Different Anti-Wrinkle Agents: A Possible Futuristic Approach

    Describes a study of microneedle patches combining acetyl octapeptide-3 with other actives that reported gains in hydration, dermal thickness and skin density. The effect of SNAP-8 alone cannot be separated. 4

  3. Review2020Lab studies and manufacturer data

    Cosmeceutical Peptides in the Framework of Sustainable Wellness Economy

    SNAP-8 mimics part of SNAP-25 and partly blocked the nerve-signal release process in lab tests (up to 43% inhibition). Its wrinkle-reduction figures come from the manufacturer. 1

Questions for your clinician

  • What treatments with stronger evidence exist for my expression lines?
  • Could anything in this product irritate my skin or clash with the skin treatments I already use?
  • Is it reasonable to use during pregnancy or breastfeeding?

Status in the US

Not approved Cosmetic ingredient; not an approved drug. It is used in cosmetics, which do not need FDA approval before sale; FDA treats products intended to change skin structure, such as removing wrinkles, as drugs or medical devices, and drugs need approval. It is not an approved drug and does not appear on FDA's list of substances nominated for pharmacy compounding (updated May 14, 2026). 236
