What’s in it

KLOW blend (GHK-Cu, BPC-157, TB-500, KPV) is a pre-mixed blend of BPC-157 and GHK-Cu and KPV and TB-500. Each part has its own fact sheet, and the blend has its own Community Signal, from reports of the blend itself.

Peptide · Healing & recoveryBPC-157A 15-amino-acid fragment of a protein first found in human stomach juice, taken for tendon, muscle and gut healing based mostly on rodent studies.Limited evidenceUnder FDA reviewPeptide · Healing & recoveryGHK-CuA small copper-binding peptide found naturally in human blood, used in skin creams and injected for skin, hair and healing, based mostly on small cosmetic and animal studies.Limited evidenceUnder FDA reviewPeptide · Gut & digestionKPVA three-amino-acid fragment of the hormone alpha-MSH, used for gut and skin inflammation on the strength of cell and animal studies; never tested in people.Animal and lab studiesUnder FDA reviewPeptide · Healing & recoveryTB-500A synthetic 7-amino-acid fragment of thymosin beta-4, promoted for injury and wound recovery; FDA found no studies of it in people.Animal and lab studiesUnder FDA review
