What it isevidence librarian

Retatrutide (LY3437943) is a lab-made peptide that switches on the receptors for three hormones: GIP, GLP-1 and glucagon. Tirzepatide acts on the first two of these; retatrutide adds glucagon. It is being tested in phase 3 trials in people with obesity, including people with type 2 diabetes or heart disease. It is not approved in the US.

Moderate evidence

The evidence is one published phase 2 randomized trial (338 adults), plus large phase 3 trials whose results so far come only from Lilly's press release. Long-term safety is not yet known.

What it may doevidence librarian

  • Moderate evidence

    Average weight loss of 21.6% to 22.6% at 80 weeks versus 3.2% with placebo in 1,949 adults with severe obesity and heart disease (phase 3 results reported by Lilly, not yet peer-reviewed) 2

  • Moderate evidence

    Average weight loss of up to 24.2% at 48 weeks versus 2.1% with placebo in a published phase 2 trial of 338 adults with obesity or overweight 3

  • Moderate evidence

    Lower blood sugar and weight in adults with type 2 diabetes and excess weight: A1C fell 1.4 to 1.6 points versus 0.2 with placebo, and weight fell up to 20.8% versus 4.0%, over 80 weeks (company-reported) 2

Risks and side effectssafety agent

  • Common

    Diarrhea, nausea, constipation, less appetite and vomiting, more often at higher doses; in one phase 3 trial diarrhea affected 27% to 34% versus 13% on placebo, and up to 13.5% stopped because of side effects versus about 5% on placebo 23

  • Uncommon

    Unpleasant or abnormal skin sensations (dysesthesia), mostly mild to moderate: 4.5% to 7.3% versus 0.7% on placebo 2

  • Not known

    Faster resting heart rate, rising with dose and peaking around 24 weeks in the phase 2 trial before easing 3

  • Not known

    Long-term safety is not known; the drug is still in trials and not approved 12

  • Not known

    Retatrutide sold online is unapproved and unregulated; FDA has sent warning letters to sellers, so contents and purity cannot be verified 1

Who should skip it, or ask a doctor first

  • Anyone outside a clinical trial: it is not an approved medicine
  • Personal or family history of medullary thyroid cancer or MEN 2 (approved drugs in this class carry this as a contraindication)
  • Pregnant, breastfeeding or planning pregnancy

What it can clash with

  • Not yet studied. A related approved drug, tirzepatide, can raise the risk of low blood sugar with insulin or sulfonylureas, can change how oral medicines are absorbed, and can make birth-control pills less effective

The main studiesevidence librarian

  1. RCT2023338 adults with obesity or overweight

    Triple–Hormone-Receptor Agonist Retatrutide for Obesity — A Phase 2 Trial

    At 48 weeks average weight change ranged from -8.7% at the lowest dose to -24.2% at the highest, versus -2.1% with placebo. Digestive side effects were most common and rose with dose; heart rate rose and peaked at 24 weeks. 3

  2. RCT20261,152 adults with type 2 diabetes and excess weight (TRIUMPH-2) and 1,949 adults with severe obesity and heart disease (TRIUMPH-3)

    Lilly's triple agonist, retatrutide, successful in two additional Phase 3 obesity trials, delivering significant improvements in weight and A1C

    At 80 weeks average weight loss reached 20.8% and 22.6% versus 4.0% and 3.2% with placebo; heart events did not differ significantly from placebo. Results are company-reported. 2

Questions to ask your doctor

  • Would a clinical trial be an option for me?
  • How do approved options such as tirzepatide or semaglutide compare for me?
  • Which side effects would mean I should stop and get checked?

Where it stands in the USsafety agent

In clinical trials Investigational; not approved, not legal to compound. Retatrutide is in phase 3 trials, and Lilly says it plans to submit it to FDA in the first quarter of 2027. FDA says it 'cannot be used in compounding under federal law' and has sent warning letters to telehealth companies, ingredient distributors and outsourcing facilities over retatrutide. 12
