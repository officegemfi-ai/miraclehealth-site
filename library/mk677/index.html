What it is

MK-677, or ibutamoren, is an experimental drug taken by mouth that mimics ghrelin, a natural hormone that triggers growth hormone release. In trials it raised growth hormone and IGF-1 in older adults to levels typical of young adults. It was studied in the 1990s and 2000s for age-related muscle loss and recovery after hip fracture.

Moderate evidence

Randomized trials in older adults show it raises growth hormone, IGF-1 and lean mass but not strength or function, sleep data come from two tiny studies, and a hip-fracture trial was stopped early over heart failure.

What it may do

  • Moderate evidence

    Raised growth hormone and IGF-1 to young-adult levels over 12 months in a randomized trial of 65 healthy adults aged 60 to 81 5

  • Moderate evidence

    Increased lean (fat-free) mass by about 1 kg versus a small loss on placebo, without gains in strength or function 5

  • Limited evidence

    Faster walking (gait speed) but no improvement in most other function measures in 123 older adults recovering from hip fracture 6

  • Limited evidence

    More deep (stage IV) and REM sleep in short studies of 8 young and 6 older adults 7

Risks and side effects

  • Not known

    Congestive heart failure: a hip-fracture trial in older adults was stopped early over a heart-failure safety signal, which FDA cites as a significant safety risk 16

  • Common

    Higher fasting blood sugar and lower insulin sensitivity over a year in older adults 5

  • Common

    Increased appetite (usually fading within a few months), mild temporary leg swelling and muscle pain, the most frequent side effects in a 2-year trial 5

  • Common

    Weight gain of about 2.7 kg over a year (vs 0.8 kg on placebo), including more fat on the arms and legs 5

Who should avoid it or check with a clinician first

  • Heart failure or other heart disease (heart-failure safety signal in older adults)
  • Diabetes or prediabetes (it raises fasting glucose and lowers insulin sensitivity)
  • Current or past cancer, unless a clinician advises otherwise (it raises IGF-1, a growth signal)
  • Pregnant or breastfeeding (no safety data)
  • Competitive athletes (prohibited by WADA)

Interactions

  • Diabetes medicines: it raises fasting glucose and lowers insulin sensitivity, which can work against glucose control

Blood markers to watch

Not an approved medicine. These are checks a clinician or a clinical trial would use, not a plan for taking it on your own.

Before starting

  • General health check with a clinician, including fasting glucose

While taking it

None routinely needed.

No evidence-based monitoring exists for MK-677; trials found raised IGF-1, higher fasting glucose and a heart-failure signal.

Key studies

  1. RCT200865 healthy adults aged 60 to 81

    Effects of an oral ghrelin mimetic on body composition and clinical outcomes in healthy older adults: A randomized trial

    Over 12 months, MK-677 raised growth hormone and IGF-1 to young-adult levels and increased fat-free mass by about 1 kg versus a small loss with placebo, without changes in strength or function. Fasting glucose rose and insulin sensitivity fell. 5

  2. RCT2011123 older adults recovering from hip fracture

    MK-0677 (ibutamoren mesylate) for the treatment of patients recovering from hip fracture: A multicenter, randomized, placebo-controlled phase IIb study

    Over 24 weeks, IGF-1 rose and gait speed improved, but most function measures did not. The trial was stopped early over a heart-failure safety signal, and the authors judged its safety profile unfavorable. 6

  3. Trial19978 adults aged 18 to 30 and 6 aged 65 to 71

    Prolonged oral treatment with MK-677, a novel growth hormone secretagogue, improves sleep quality in man

    Over 1 to 2 weeks, deep sleep rose by about half and REM sleep by more than a fifth versus placebo in young adults; REM sleep rose by nearly half in older adults, whose part may not have been blinded. 7

Questions for your clinician

  • Given FDA's heart-failure concern, should I avoid it?
  • How would it affect my blood sugar, and should we check glucose or HbA1c?
  • If my goal is strength, which approaches improve it, since MK-677 added lean mass but not strength?

Status in the US

FDA safety-risk list FDA Category 2: pharmacies may not compound it. FDA lists ibutamoren mesylate in Category 2 for both compounding pharmacies (since September 2023) and outsourcing facilities (since December 2022), citing a risk of congestive heart failure, and it remained there on FDA's 2025 and 2026 lists. WADA bans ibutamoren (MK-677) in sport. 1234
