What it isevidence librarian

Testosterone is an androgen, a male sex hormone. Together with dihydrotestosterone (DHT), it is responsible for the normal growth and development of the male sex organs and for maintaining male secondary sex characteristics. Prescription testosterone replaces it in men whose testes fail, or whose pituitary or hypothalamus does not signal the testes properly. It comes in several forms, including gels applied to the skin.

Strong evidence

It is FDA-approved for male hypogonadism. Large randomized trials (TRAVERSE, 5,246 men; the Testosterone Trials, 790 men) describe its heart safety and its modest benefits in older men.

What it may doevidence librarian

  • Strong evidence

    Replaces missing testosterone in men with hypogonadism caused by testicular failure (such as Klinefelter syndrome, chemotherapy or removal of the testes) or by pituitary or hypothalamic disease 1

  • Moderate evidence

    Modest gains in sexual activity, desire and erectile function, and small improvements in mood and depressive symptoms, over one year in 790 men 65 and older with low testosterone; no benefit for vitality or walking distance 3

  • Moderate evidence

    About 1.6 kg less body fat and 1.6 kg more lean (fat-free) mass, with no change in body weight, in middle-aged and older men (29 randomized trials, 1,083 men, average age 65, average starting testosterone low-normal) 8

  • Strong evidence

    Better sexual desire, arousal, orgasm and satisfying sexual events in postmenopausal women with low sexual desire causing distress (meta-analysis of 36 trials, 8,480 women); off-label in the US 4

Risks and side effectssafety agent

  • Common

    Prostate effects: PSA rose in 11.1% versus 0% on placebo in a gel trial, and an enlarged prostate can worsen; men with prostate cancer should not use it 15

  • Uncommon

    Too many red blood cells (high hematocrit), which thickens the blood; the Endocrine Society advises holding treatment if hematocrit goes above 54% 153

  • Common

    Lower sperm production and possible infertility, because added testosterone suppresses LH and FSH, the signals that drive the testes 156

  • Not known

    Higher blood pressure: FDA says blood-pressure monitoring studies confirmed increases with all testosterone products 21

  • Uncommon

    More atrial fibrillation (3.5% versus 2.4%), acute kidney injury (2.3% versus 1.5%), lung clots (0.9% versus 0.5%) and fractures (3.5% versus 2.5%) than placebo in the TRAVERSE trial 17

  • Not known

    Boxed warning for gels: male-type changes (virilization) in children exposed to skin where the gel was applied 1

Who should skip it, or ask a doctor first

  • Men planning to father a child soon
  • Breast cancer, prostate cancer, or an unexplained prostate lump or high PSA
  • High hematocrit, untreated severe sleep apnea or severe urinary symptoms
  • Uncontrolled heart failure, or a heart attack or stroke in the past 6 months
  • Pregnancy (it can masculinize a female fetus)

What it can clash with

  • Insulin and other diabetes medicines: testosterone may lower blood sugar
  • Warfarin and other oral blood thinners: their effect can change, so INR needs checking
  • Corticosteroids: more fluid retention

Blood tests to watchmarkers agent

Before starting

  • Morning fasting total testosterone on two separate days
  • LH and FSH
  • Hematocrit
  • PSA and prostate exam

While taking it

  • Testosterone and hematocrit at 3 to 6 months, at 12 months, then yearly
  • PSA and prostate exam 3 to 12 months after starting, then routine screening
  • Blood pressure and lipids periodically

Tests confirm the diagnosis before starting, then watch for high hematocrit (treatment is held above 54%), PSA rises that need a urologist, and blood pressure or cholesterol changes, as the label and the Endocrine Society guideline advise.

The main studiesevidence librarian

  1. Meta-analysis20198,480 women in 36 randomized trials

    Safety and efficacy of testosterone for women: a systematic review and meta-analysis of randomised controlled trial data

    Testosterone improved sexual desire, arousal, orgasm, pleasure and satisfying sexual events in postmenopausal women. Oral forms worsened cholesterol, and acne and hair growth were more common; no serious adverse events were recorded in these trials, which were mostly short, so long-term safety is unknown. 4

  2. RCT20235,246 men aged 45 to 80 with low testosterone and heart disease or high heart risk

    Cardiovascular Safety of Testosterone-Replacement Therapy

    Major heart events occurred in 7.0% with testosterone versus 7.3% with placebo (hazard ratio 0.96), meeting the trial's noninferiority goal; atrial fibrillation, acute kidney injury and lung clots were more common with testosterone. 7

  3. RCT2016790 men aged 65 and older with low testosterone

    Effects of Testosterone Treatment in Older Men

    One year of testosterone gel modestly improved sexual function and slightly improved mood and depressive symptoms, but not vitality or walking distance; the trial was too small to judge risks firmly. 3

  4. Meta-analysis20051,083 men in 29 randomized trials (average age 65)

    Effects of testosterone on body composition, bone metabolism and serum lipid profile in middle-aged men: a meta-analysis

    Testosterone lowered total body fat by about 1.6 kg (about 6%) and raised fat-free mass by about 1.6 kg (about 3%) compared with controls, with no change in body weight. 8

Questions to ask your doctor

  • Do my symptoms and two morning tests confirm low testosterone, and what is causing it?
  • How would this affect my fertility, and what are my options if I want children?
  • How often will you check my hematocrit, PSA and blood pressure?

Where it stands in the USsafety agent

FDA-approved FDA-approved for male hypogonadism; Schedule III. Approved for men with low testosterone from testicular failure or pituitary/hypothalamic causes; benefit is not established for 'age-related' low testosterone, and men's products are labeled not for use in women. In February 2025 FDA had all makers add the TRAVERSE results, remove heart-risk language from the boxed warning and add a blood-pressure warning; AndroGel's label keeps a boxed warning about transfer of the gel to children. 12
