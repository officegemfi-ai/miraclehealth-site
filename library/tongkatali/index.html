What it is

Tongkat ali (Eurycoma longifolia) is a tall, slender, shrubby tree native to Southeast Asia, including Malaysia, Thailand, Laos, Cambodia and Myanmar. Its roots and bark have been used in traditional medicine for fever, malaria, coughs and many other conditions, and especially as an aphrodisiac. Its effects are attributed mainly to compounds called quassinoids, such as eurycomanone.

Limited evidence

A 2022 meta-analysis of 5 small trials found higher total testosterone, mainly in men with low testosterone, but results varied widely and the trials were few and small.

What it may do

  • Limited evidence

    Higher total testosterone than control in a 2022 meta-analysis of small trials; the rise was significant in men with low testosterone but not in men with normal levels, and results varied widely between trials 2

  • Limited evidence

    No clear benefit shown for erectile function versus placebo in 2 randomized trials (139 men) at high risk of bias; an older review counted benefit in 7 of 11 small studies of male sexual health 13

  • Limited evidence

    Better stress-hormone profile and some mood scores over 4 weeks in one study of 63 adults with moderate stress 1

Risks and side effects

  • Not known

    Little is known about safety: trials were few and small, and side effects are poorly documented 12

  • Rare

    Liver injury: it was among the single-ingredient supplements implicated in a US liver-injury network analysis 1

  • In theory

    Hormonal effects: it can raise testosterone, and in rats it enlarged the prostate; it should not be used with prostate cancer 12

  • Not known

    Contamination: one Malaysian product had higher-than-normal lead levels 1

  • Not known

    Hidden drugs: FDA says supplements found to contain hidden prescription drugs, controlled substances or untested drug ingredients are typically promoted for sexual enhancement, weight loss, bodybuilding or diabetes 4

Who should avoid it or check with a clinician first

  • Pregnant or breastfeeding
  • Prostate cancer
  • Liver disease (clinician first)
  • Taking propranolol (clinician first)

Interactions

  • Propranolol (a beta-blocker): tongkat ali may lower its blood levels

Blood markers to watch

Before starting

  • Total testosterone, if you are taking it for suspected low testosterone

While taking it

None routinely needed.

A testosterone test shows whether low testosterone is really the issue; yellow skin or eyes, dark urine or unusual tiredness should prompt liver tests.

Key studies

  1. Meta-analysis2022Men in 5 trials pooled as 7 comparisons (135 supplemented, 132 controls), with low or normal testosterone

    Eurycoma longifolia (Jack) Improves Serum Total Testosterone in Men: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis of Clinical Trials

    Total testosterone rose overall and in men with low testosterone, but not significantly in men with normal levels; differences between trials were very large. 2

  2. Systematic review201711 human studies of male sexual health published from 2000 to 2014

    Eurycoma Longifolia as a potential adoptogen [sic] of male sexual health: a systematic review on clinical studies

    Seven studies reported a notable benefit and four did not; most were small randomized trials, cohort studies or pilot trials. 3

Questions for your clinician

  • Should my testosterone be tested before I try this, and is there an underlying cause to look for?
  • Is it safe with my prostate history or my medicines, including propranolol?
  • Which symptoms should make me stop and get liver tests?

Status in the US

Dietary supplement Dietary supplement. Sold as a dietary supplement. A professional monograph notes that FDA has not reviewed it for safety or effectiveness and does not consider it generally recognized as safe (GRAS), a status for food ingredients that supplements are not required to have. 1
