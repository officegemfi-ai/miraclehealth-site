Be one of the first 100.

100 spots

Every Community Signal starts from what people say on public forums. Each first-hand report from a member moves it toward what members actually experienced. The first 100 members to share a first-hand report become founding reporters.

What a founding reporter gets

  • The Founding reporter badge on your profile, for good.
  • Your report in the first community ratings: ten members’ reports unlock the community’s own rating for each compound and use.

What counts

  • Something you took yourself: what for, how long, and how it compares with before you started.
  • Better, about the same or worse all count the same. A report that says it didn’t work is just as useful.
  • Sig reads it into the Signal, and you check its read with a tap. Questions don’t count.
