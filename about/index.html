About

No miracles. Just what happened.

Updated Nov 5, 2026

Miracle Health is a free place to look up peptides, hormones, supplements and medications, ask about them, and say how they went. It has three parts, kept apart and always named: the Library, where each fact sheet is checked against its sources; the community, where members share first-hand reports; and Ask Sig, where Sig, Miracle Health’s AI, answers from both.

Who runs it

Miracle Health is independent and run by its founder. It isn’t a clinic, a pharmacy or a seller, and nobody here can examine you, diagnose you or prescribe anything. Write to us at hello@miraclehealth.us; a person reads every email.

How it’s paid for

It’s free for everyone, and today it’s paid for by its founder. There are no ads, no sponsors and no affiliate links, and nobody can pay to be in the Library, in Sig’s answers or in the Community Signal. We never sell what members share.

If Miracle Health ever sells anything of its own, it will be kept apart: never in the Library, never in Sig’s answers, never counted in the Signal. And this page will say so before it happens.

How fact sheets are checked

Each fact sheet is checked claim by claim against sources we opened: FDA labels and documents, NIH fact sheets and published trials. Every benefit says what kind of evidence backs it, from randomized trials in people to studies in rats, and every claim links to its source. Each sheet shows the date it was checked; 122 of 300 compounds have one so far, and the rest say they’re still being checked. How the numbers are made explains the grades, how reports are read and weighted, and the side-effect watch.

What we never do

  • No doses, amounts, schedules or how to take or inject anything, for any compound.
  • No sellers, brands, prices or where to get something.
  • No diagnosis, and never start, stop or change a prescribed medicine: that’s for your doctor.
  • No health records: Sig keeps nothing you write, and the community holds only what members choose to post.

Corrections

Found a mistake? Tell us which page and what’s wrong, with a source if you have one. When we fix a fact sheet, its “checked” date changes, and significant corrections are listed here.
