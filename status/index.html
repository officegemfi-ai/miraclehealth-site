Where each compound stands with the FDA

Checked Oct 28, 2026 · 122 compounds

Whether a compound is approved, can be compounded by a pharmacy, or is on FDA’s list of substances with significant safety risks, compound by compound, from the Library’s fact sheets. It’s moving fast this year: each line says when we last checked it, and links to the FDA documents behind it.

What the words mean
FDA-approved
FDA has approved a product for at least one use. Using it for anything else is off-label, which a prescriber can choose to do.
Compounding
A pharmacy making a medicine for a patient’s prescription. Pharmacies (503A) and outsourcing facilities (503B) follow different rules.
Category 1
On FDA’s interim policy, substances still being evaluated, which licensed pharmacies may compound under set conditions while FDA decides.
Category 2
Substances FDA says raise significant safety risks when compounded.
Category 3
Substances nominated without enough information for FDA to evaluate them.
Dietary supplement
Sold without FDA approval; the maker is responsible for its safety and labeling.
