Side by side

Semax vs Selank

From each fact sheet · checked Oct 28, 2026

What each one is, its status, what the studies show, its risks and what people who took it report, from their fact sheets, each claim checked against its source. It isn’t a recommendation: which one fits, if either, is for you and your doctor.

SemaxAlso called ACTH 4-10 analog
Selank

What it is

Semax

A lab-made peptide based on a piece of the hormone ACTH. Registered in Russia as nasal drops, and taken for focus, memory and stroke recovery.

Selank

A small lab-made peptide based on tuftsin, a piece of an immune protein. Taken for anxiety and focus. The evidence in people is one small Russian study.

Status in the US

Semax

Under FDA review Not FDA-approved; compounding decision pending.

Selank

Not approved Not approved; not eligible for compounding.

Evidence overall

Semax

Limited evidence

Rat studies show effects on brain growth factors. But the human data are a few small Russian studies, and FDA judged the evidence insufficient for stroke, migraine and nerve pain.

Selank

Limited evidence

The only human evidence we found is one Russian comparative study of 62 patients. In it, selank eased anxiety about as much as the drug medazepam. The other data come from rat studies.

Mostly taken for

Semax

Focus & mood

Selank

Focus & mood

What it may do

Semax
  • Limited evidenceHigher blood BDNF and faster functional gains in a Russian study of 110 adults in stroke rehabilitation; whether the study was randomized isn't clear
  • Animal and lab studiesRaised BDNF and its receptor in the hippocampus and improved avoidance learning in rats
  • Animal and lab studiesCountered the drop in brain growth-factor genes after reduced blood flow to the brain in rats
Selank
  • Limited evidenceEased anxiety about as much as the anti-anxiety drug medazepam in a study of 62 adults with generalized anxiety disorder or neurasthenia, with added anti-fatigue and mildly stimulating effects
  • Animal and lab studiesChanged the activity of GABA receptor genes in the rat brain within hours of a single dose, which researchers suggest could explain calming effects

Risks and side effects

Semax
  • In theoryPossible immune reactions to impurities or clumped peptide in unregulated products
  • In theoryBoosted amphetamine-triggered dopamine release in mice; abuse potential has not been studied
  • In theoryAnti-clotting effects in animal studies; bleeding risk in people is unclear
  • Not knownNo standard toxicity studies, including reproductive safety, were found by FDA
Selank
  • In theoryPossible immune reactions, because the peptide can clump and carry peptide-related impurities, according to FDA
  • Not knownHuman safety is essentially unstudied: FDA says important safety information is lacking, and the clinical study abstract reports no side-effect data
  • In theoryResearchers describe marked effects on the immune system, with unknown consequences in people

Skip it, or ask a doctor first, if

Semax
  • Pregnant or breastfeeding (no reproductive safety data)
  • Bleeding disorder or taking blood thinners
  • Taking stimulant medicines, or a history of stimulant misuse
Selank
  • Pregnant or breastfeeding (no safety data)
  • Taking benzodiazepines or other sedatives, unless a doctor advises (it is thought to act on the same GABA system)
  • Autoimmune disease or a weakened immune system, unless a doctor advises (it may affect immune function)

Interactions

Semax
  • Amphetamine and other stimulants (semax increased amphetamine-induced dopamine release in mice; not studied in people)
  • Blood thinners and antiplatelet drugs (anti-clotting effects seen in animals; not studied in people)
Selank
  • Benzodiazepines and other sedatives: it is thought to act on the same GABA system, so check with a doctor
