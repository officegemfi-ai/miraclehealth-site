Side by side

Sildenafil vs Tadalafil

From each fact sheet · checked Oct 28, 2026

What each one is, its status, what the studies show, its risks and what people who took it report, from their fact sheets, each claim checked against its source. It isn’t a recommendation: which one fits, if either, is for you and your doctor.

SildenafilAlso called Viagra, Revatio
TadalafilAlso called Cialis

What it is

Sildenafil

The prescription pill in Viagra (a PDE5 inhibitor), for erectile dysfunction. The same drug is also sold as Revatio for pulmonary arterial hypertension (high blood pressure in the lungs).

Tadalafil

A prescription pill (a PDE5 inhibitor) for erectile dysfunction and for urinary symptoms of an enlarged prostate. In trials, its effect on erections lasted up to 36 hours.

Status in the US

Sildenafil

FDA-approved FDA-approved for erectile dysfunction.

Tadalafil

FDA-approved FDA-approved for ED and enlarged-prostate symptoms.

Evidence overall

Sildenafil

Strong evidence

It was FDA-approved for erectile dysfunction after 21 randomized trials in more than 3,000 men. The AUA recommends telling men with ED about PDE5 inhibitors, and finds the drugs in the class similarly effective.

Tadalafil

Strong evidence

It is FDA-approved for erectile dysfunction and for urinary symptoms of benign prostatic hyperplasia, based on randomized trials. AUA guidelines recommend PDE5 inhibitors for ED and list tadalafil as an option for these urinary symptoms.

Mostly taken for

Sildenafil

Sexual health

Tadalafil

Sexual health

What it may do

Sildenafil
  • Strong evidenceImproved erections in men aged 19 to 87 with erectile dysfunction of many causes: in pooled fixed-dose studies, 63% to 82% reported better erections versus 24% on placebo
  • Strong evidenceWorked better than placebo in all 21 randomized trials in its approval program (more than 3,000 men)
  • Limited evidenceIn men with ED and testosterone deficiency confirmed by a doctor, the AUA says treating the deficiency may improve results (low-certainty evidence)
Tadalafil
  • Strong evidenceImproved erections in men with erectile dysfunction: in two US trials, successful intercourse attempts rose to 50% and 64% versus 25% and 23% on placebo, with effects lasting up to 36 hours
  • Strong evidenceTreats urinary symptoms of benign prostatic hyperplasia, with or without erectile dysfunction; the AUA lists it as a treatment option
  • Moderate evidenceImproved erectile function compared with placebo in 216 men with type 1 or type 2 diabetes
  • Limited evidenceIn men with ED and testosterone deficiency confirmed by a doctor, the AUA says treating the deficiency may improve results (low-certainty evidence)

Risks and side effects

Sildenafil
  • CommonHeadache (16% versus 4% on placebo), flushing (10% versus 1%), indigestion, nasal congestion, back and muscle pain, nausea, dizziness and rash
  • UncommonVision changes, mainly a color tinge or blurring
  • Not knownSevere drop in blood pressure with nitrates or riociguat; added blood pressure lowering with alpha-blockers and other blood pressure drugs
  • RareSudden vision loss from NAION (damage to the optic nerve) and sudden hearing loss have been reported rarely; the label says to stop it and get medical care right away if either happens, and people who have had NAION before are at higher risk of it happening again
Tadalafil
  • CommonHeadache (about 11% to 15% versus 5% on placebo), indigestion, back pain, muscle aches, nasal congestion, flushing and limb pain
  • Not knownSevere drop in blood pressure if combined with nitrates or riociguat; added blood pressure lowering with alpha-blockers, other blood pressure drugs and heavy drinking
  • Not knownErection lasting more than 4 hours (priapism), which needs emergency care
  • RareSudden vision loss from NAION (damage to the optic nerve) and sudden hearing loss have been reported rarely; the label says to stop it and get medical care right away if either happens, and people who have had NAION before are at higher risk of it happening again

Skip it, or ask a doctor first, if

Sildenafil
  • Taking nitrates or other nitric oxide donors in any form, including the street drugs called "poppers" (contraindicated)
  • Taking riociguat or another guanylate cyclase stimulator (contraindicated)
  • Recent heart attack, stroke or life-threatening arrhythmia, or other serious heart disease, unless a doctor confirms sexual activity is safe
  • Already taking Revatio or another PDE5 inhibitor
Tadalafil
  • Taking nitrates in any form, regularly or now and then (contraindicated)
  • Taking riociguat or another guanylate cyclase stimulator (contraindicated)
  • Heart conditions that make sexual activity unsafe, such as unstable angina or uncontrolled arrhythmia or blood pressure
  • Severe liver impairment

Interactions

Sildenafil
  • Nitrates and other nitric oxide donors: contraindicated because of a dangerous drop in blood pressure
  • Alpha-blockers: added blood pressure lowering; the label advises that patients be stable on the alpha-blocker first
  • Ritonavir: raises sildenafil levels about 11-fold; erythromycin and saquinavir also raise levels substantially
  • Amlodipine and other blood pressure drugs: added blood pressure lowering
Tadalafil
  • Nitrates: contraindicated; tadalafil lasts longer than other ED drugs, so if a nitrate is ever needed in an emergency, medical staff need to know when it was last taken
  • Alpha-blockers (such as doxazosin, tamsulosin or alfuzosin) and other blood pressure drugs: extra blood pressure lowering; for BPH, combining with an alpha-blocker is not recommended
  • Strong CYP3A4 inhibitors (ketoconazole, ritonavir, itraconazole; grapefruit juice likely): raise tadalafil levels
  • CYP3A4 inducers such as rifampin: sharply lower tadalafil levels
