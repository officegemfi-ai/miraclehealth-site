Miracle AI

Five agents ready

Talk it through with Miracle AI.

Ask what the evidence says about a peptide or supplement, or how to work toward a goal. Like a good clinician, Miracle AI asks a few questions so the answer fits you, and five agents check every answer against the Library’s fact sheets and the Community Signal.

Who answers

  1. Safety agentScreens every message for emergencies, signs of crisis, medicines and conditions, and reads every reply again before it stands.
  2. RecommenderAsks what it needs to know, like a clinician taking a history, then finds options in the Library’s fact sheets and the Community Signal and explains the evidence.
  3. Synergy auditorChecks compounds together with your medicines and conditions: overlaps, risks that add up, interactions.
  4. Stack builderLays out your stack, and options to discuss with a clinician. Never doses, never sellers.
  5. Clinician reviewDone by a licensed clinician who knows you: take them your review packet, then mark it reviewed. Until then, nothing here is a plan.

For adults 18 and over. Miracle AI explains evidence; it doesn’t diagnose, give doses or name sellers. Your chat stays in this browser tab; messages go to our AI provider to write the answers and aren’t stored by Miracle Health. How messages are handled
