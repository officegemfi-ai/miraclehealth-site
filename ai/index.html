Ask Sig

one question · every answer checked

sig · 10 agents + the community ready · Library updated Nov 7, 2026

No doses. No sellers. No diagnosis. The Safety agent checks every answer before you see it.

Ask anything. Get the studies and the people, kept apart.

I’m Sig, Miracle Health’s AI. Ask me about a goal, something you take, or something you’re thinking about. I’ll tell you what the studies show and what people report, and I’ll always say which is which. No doses, no sellers, no diagnosis. Your doctor has the final say.

›
Who answers
  1. Safety agentReads your message first, and checks every answer before you see it.
  2. PlannerTurns your goal into what studies measure. “Lose fat” becomes body fat, waist and lean mass.
  3. RecommenderAsks what it needs to know, then reads the fact sheets in the Library.
  4. Evidence librarianFinds every study result on exactly that: how strong it is, and who was studied.
  5. Relatives agentFinds things that work through the same hormone or receptor, like testosterone for enclomiphene, and says whose evidence it is.
  6. Mechanism agentExplains how each one could affect your goal, from its fact sheet. That is theory, not a test result, and it says so.
  7. The communityWhat members report first-hand and what public forums say, as totals. It leads where the studies run out.
  8. Combination agentSays whether the things you take together overlap or work in different ways.
  9. Markers agentFlags blood tests that two things push in opposite directions, like IGF-1 with HGH and enclomiphene.
  10. Synergy auditorChecks everything together with your medicines and conditions.
  11. Stack builderLays out what you take and the options to discuss with your doctor. Never doses, never sellers.

For adults 18 and over. Sig is an AI. It explains evidence; it doesn’t diagnose, give doses or name sellers. Your chat stays in this browser tab. Messages go to our AI provider to write the answers and aren’t stored by Miracle Health. How messages are handled
