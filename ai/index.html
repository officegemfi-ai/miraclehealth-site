I’m Sig, Miracle Health’s AI. I answer with what studies show and what members and the public forums report, and say which is which. Where the studies run out, the community’s reports lead. Your own clinician has the final say.

Who answers
  1. Safety agentScreens your message first, and checks every reply before you see it.
  2. PlannerTurns what you want into what studies measure: fat loss is body fat, waist and lean mass.
  3. RecommenderAsks what it needs to know, then reads the studies: the Library’s fact sheets.
  4. Evidence librarianFinds every finding on exactly that, how strong it is and who was studied.
  5. Relatives agentFinds what works through the same hormone or receptor, like testosterone for enclomiphene, and says whose evidence it is.
  6. Mechanism agentReads how each one could bear on your goal, from its fact sheet, as expected, not tested.
  7. The communityWhat members report first-hand and the public forums say, in totals. It leads where the studies run out.
  8. Combination agentSays whether what you take together overlaps or works through different pathways.
  9. Markers agentFlags blood markers two compounds push opposite ways, like IGF-1 with HGH and enclomiphene.
  10. Synergy auditorChecks compounds together with your medicines and conditions.
  11. Stack builderLays out your stack and options to discuss. Never doses, never sellers.
