Gut & inflammation.

7 compounds studied for it · 1 with strong or moderate evidence

What the studies show for each compound people take for gut & inflammation, strongest evidence first, next to its Community Signal: what people who took it report. It isn’t a ranking of what to take: the evidence grade is for the compound as a whole, and each fact sheet says what it was studied for.

Studied for gut & inflammation

Supplement · Gut & digestion Probiotics Live microorganisms, such as Lactobacillus, Bifidobacterium and Saccharomyces boulardii. Taken mainly to prevent diarrhea from antibiotics and to ease gut symptoms. Moderate evidenceDietary supplement Peptide · Healing & recovery BPC-157 A small piece of a protein first found in human stomach juice. People take it to heal tendons, muscles and the gut. Most of the evidence comes from rodents. Limited evidenceUnder FDA review Supplement · Immune & inflammation Curcumin The main yellow compound in turmeric, taken mostly for joint pain and inflammation. The evidence is still early, and turmeric supplements have been linked to rare liver injury. Limited evidenceDietary supplement Peptide · Liver & detox Glutathione An antioxidant your body makes. Taken by mouth or by IV for liver health, detox and skin lightening. Studies in people are small, and IV use has caused serious reactions. Limited evidenceCompounded by prescription Supplement · Liver & detox NAC (N-acetylcysteine) A modified form of the amino acid cysteine. Approved as an antidote for acetaminophen overdose and as a mucus thinner, and sold for decades as a dietary supplement. Limited evidenceRestricted Peptide · Gut & digestion KPV A tiny piece of the hormone alpha-MSH, used for gut and skin inflammation. That rests on cell and animal studies. It has never been tested in people. Animal and lab studiesUnder FDA review Peptide · Liver & detox Ovagen A short lab-made peptide from Russian 'bioregulator' research, promoted for the liver and digestive tract. Exactly what it is, and whether it helps people, are poorly documented. Animal and lab studiesNot approved

Sometimes taken for it

Studied mainly for something else, with some evidence or interest for gut & inflammation.

Supplement · Heart & circulation Omega-3 fish oil Oils from fish, krill or algae that supply the omega-3 fats EPA and DHA. Taken mainly for heart health and to lower triglycerides. Strong evidenceDietary supplement Peptide · Metabolic & weight Semaglutide The prescription GLP-1 medicine sold as Ozempic, Rybelsus and Wegovy. Used to lower blood sugar in type 2 diabetes and to lose weight. Strong evidenceFDA-approved Peptide · Metabolic & weight Mazdutide A drug that acts on two hormone receptors, GLP-1 and glucagon. Approved in China in 2025 for weight management and type 2 diabetes. Not approved in the US. Moderate evidenceIn clinical trials Peptide · Metabolic & weight Survodutide An experimental Boehringer Ingelheim drug that acts on two hormone receptors, glucagon and GLP-1. In phase 3 trials for obesity and fatty-liver disease. Not FDA-approved. Moderate evidenceIn clinical trials Peptide · Growth-hormone axis Ipamorelin A small lab-made peptide taken to raise growth hormone, acting on the ghrelin receptor. Its only treatment trial, for bowel recovery after surgery, missed its main goal. Limited evidenceFDA safety-risk list Supplement · Cognition & focus Lion’s mane An edible mushroom (Hericium erinaceus) taken for memory, focus and mood. Small trials in people show mixed results, and most of the evidence comes from lab and animal studies. Limited evidenceDietary supplement Medication · Immune & inflammation Low-dose naltrexone (LDN) Naltrexone is an opioid-blocking drug for addiction. In much smaller amounts, people take it for chronic pain and inflammatory conditions, which it isn't approved for. Small trials have given mixed results. Limited evidenceCompounded by prescription Supplement · Longevity NR (nicotinamide riboside) A form of vitamin B3 that raises NAD+, a molecule cells use for energy. Taken in hopes of healthier aging. Trials show few clear health effects. Limited evidenceDietary supplement Peptide · Metabolic & weight Pancragen A tiny peptide from Russian 'bioregulator' research, promoted for the pancreas and blood sugar. Tested in cells, monkeys and small studies in people. Limited evidenceNot approved Peptide · Metabolic & weight Pemvidutide An experimental drug that acts on two hormone receptors, glucagon and GLP-1. Tested for weight loss, and now mainly for fatty-liver disease (MASH) and alcohol use disorder. Not FDA-approved. Limited evidenceIn clinical trials Peptide · Healing & recovery Pentadeca Arginate A peptide described as a form of the experimental peptide BPC-157 (its arginine salt), used for injury healing. It has not been tested in published studies. Not FDA-approved. Animal and lab studiesStatus unclear
