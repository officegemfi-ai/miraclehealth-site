Energy & mitochondria.

9 compounds studied for it · 2 with strong or moderate evidence

What the studies show for each compound people take for energy & mitochondria, strongest evidence first, next to its Community Signal: what people who took it report. It isn’t a ranking of what to take: the evidence grade is for the compound as a whole, and each fact sheet says what it was studied for.

Studied for energy & mitochondria

Supplement · Vitamins & minerals Iron An essential mineral the body needs to make hemoglobin. Taken to prevent or treat iron deficiency and the anemia and tiredness it can cause. Strong evidenceDietary supplement Supplement · Vitamins & minerals Vitamin B12 An essential vitamin for nerves, red blood cells and DNA. Taken to prevent or treat deficiency, which is more likely with age, vegan diets and some medicines. Strong evidenceDietary supplement Supplement · Energy & mitochondria CoQ10 A substance your body makes that helps cells produce energy. Taken for heart health, muscle aches from statins, migraine and energy. Limited evidenceDietary supplement Medication · Energy & mitochondria Methylene blue A blue dye that is also an approved hospital drug for a rare blood disorder. Now promoted for focus and energy. The evidence in people for those uses is thin. Limited evidenceFDA-approved Supplement · Energy & mitochondria NAD+ A molecule your cells use to make energy. Given by IV drip or injection, or taken by mouth, by people hoping for more energy and slower aging. The evidence in people is minimal. Limited evidenceCompounded by prescription Supplement · Mood & stress Rhodiola rosea A cold-climate herb (golden root) taken to fight fatigue and stress and to lift mood. Trials are small and conflicting, and NIH says there isn't enough evidence. Limited evidenceDietary supplement Peptide · Longevity SS-31 A lab-made peptide that targets mitochondria, where cells make energy. FDA-approved in 2025 as Forzinity, for Barth syndrome only. People also take it for aging and energy, based mostly on animal studies. Limited evidenceFDA-approved Supplement · Longevity Taurine An amino acid found in energy drinks and supplements, taken for energy and healthy aging. The lifespan gains seen in animals haven't been shown in people. Limited evidenceDietary supplement Medication · Metabolic & weight SLU-PP-332 An experimental compound that copies some effects of exercise in mice by switching on estrogen-related receptors (ERRs). No studies in people have been published. Animal and lab studiesNot approved

Sometimes taken for it

Studied mainly for something else, with some evidence or interest for energy & mitochondria.

Supplement · Muscle & performance Creatine monohydrate A compound your body makes and also gets from animal foods. Taken as a supplement to add strength and power for short, intense exercise. Strong evidenceDietary supplement Hormone · Hormone balance Testosterone A male sex hormone. Prescribed to men whose testosterone is low because of a problem with the testicles, the pituitary gland or the hypothalamus. A Schedule III controlled substance in the US. Strong evidenceFDA-approved Medication · Cognition & focus Modafinil A prescription drug that keeps people awake, for narcolepsy, sleep apnea and shift-work sleepiness. Also taken for focus, which it isn't approved for. In rested, healthy people its effects are small. Moderate evidenceFDA-approved Medication · Cognition & focus Armodafinil One of the two mirror-image forms of modafinil, a prescription drug that keeps people with sleep disorders awake. Also taken for focus, an unapproved use with little direct evidence in healthy people. Limited evidenceFDA-approved Supplement · Mood & stress Ashwagandha An herb from Ayurvedic medicine (Withania somnifera). Extracts of its root are taken mainly to ease stress and anxiety and to sleep better. Limited evidenceDietary supplement Peptide · Immune & inflammation Crystagen A tiny peptide from Russian 'bioregulator' research, promoted to support immunity in older people. The evidence is mainly small studies by its own developers. Limited evidenceNot approved Supplement · Vitamins & minerals Magnesium glycinate The mineral magnesium bound to the amino acid glycine. Taken mainly for sleep and relaxation, and to top up magnesium intake. Limited evidenceDietary supplement Supplement · Longevity NMN A building block your body uses to make NAD+ (full name: nicotinamide mononucleotide). Taken in hopes of slowing aging and boosting metabolism. Benefits in people are unproven. Limited evidenceDietary supplement Supplement · Longevity NR (nicotinamide riboside) A form of vitamin B3 that raises NAD+, a molecule cells use for energy. Taken in hopes of healthier aging. Trials show few clear health effects. Limited evidenceDietary supplement Peptide · Longevity MOTS-c A small peptide coded for in the DNA of mitochondria. People take it hoping to improve metabolism, exercise capacity and aging. Its effects have only been tested in rodents and cells. Animal and lab studiesUnder FDA review
