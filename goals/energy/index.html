Energy & mitochondria

9 compounds studied for it · 2 with strong or moderate evidence

What the studies show for each compound people take for energy & mitochondria, strongest evidence first, next to its Community Signal: what people who took it report. It isn’t a ranking of what to take: the evidence grade is for the compound as a whole, and each fact sheet says what it was studied for.

Studied for energy & mitochondria

Supplement · Vitamins & minerals Iron An essential mineral needed to make hemoglobin, taken to prevent or treat iron deficiency and the anemia and fatigue it can cause. Strong evidenceDietary supplement Supplement · Vitamins & minerals Vitamin B12 An essential vitamin for nerves, red blood cells and DNA, taken to prevent or treat deficiency, which is more likely with age, vegan diets and some medicines. Strong evidenceDietary supplement Supplement · Energy & mitochondria CoQ10 A substance the body makes that helps cells produce energy, taken for heart health, statin-related muscle aches, migraine and energy. Limited evidenceDietary supplement Medication · Energy & mitochondria Methylene blue A blue dye and approved hospital drug for a rare blood disorder, now promoted for focus and energy; human evidence for those uses is thin. Limited evidenceFDA-approved Supplement · Energy & mitochondria NAD+ A molecule cells use to make energy, given by IV drip or injection or taken by mouth by people hoping for more energy and slower aging; human evidence is minimal. Limited evidenceCompounded by prescription Supplement · Mood & stress Rhodiola rosea A cold-climate herb (golden root) taken to fight fatigue and stress and lift mood; trials are small and conflicting, and NIH says the evidence is insufficient. Limited evidenceDietary supplement Peptide · Longevity SS-31 A lab-made peptide that targets mitochondria, FDA-approved in 2025 as Forzinity for Barth syndrome and taken off-label for aging and energy based mostly on animal studies. Limited evidenceFDA-approved Supplement · Longevity Taurine An amino acid found in energy drinks and supplements, taken for energy and healthy aging; lifespan gains seen in animals haven't been shown in people. Limited evidenceDietary supplement Medication · Metabolic & weight SLU-PP-332 An experimental compound that switches on estrogen-related receptors (ERRs) and mimics some effects of exercise in mice; no human studies have been published. Sometimes taken for it

Studied mainly for something else, with some evidence or interest for energy & mitochondria.

Supplement · Muscle & performance Creatine monohydrate A compound the body makes and gets from animal foods, taken as a supplement to add strength and power for short, intense exercise. Strong evidenceDietary supplement Hormone · Hormone balance Testosterone A male sex hormone (androgen) prescribed to men with low testosterone from testicular, pituitary or hypothalamic problems; a Schedule III controlled substance in the US. Strong evidenceFDA-approved Medication · Cognition & focus Modafinil A prescription wakefulness drug for narcolepsy, sleep apnea and shift work sleepiness, used off-label for focus; in rested healthy people its effects are small. Moderate evidenceFDA-approved Medication · Cognition & focus Armodafinil The R-form of modafinil: a prescription wakefulness drug for sleep disorders, used off-label for focus with little direct evidence in healthy people. Limited evidenceFDA-approved Supplement · Mood & stress Ashwagandha An Ayurvedic herb (Withania somnifera) whose root extracts are taken mainly to ease stress and anxiety and to improve sleep. Limited evidenceDietary supplement Peptide · Immune & inflammation Crystagen A three-amino-acid peptide (Glu-Asp-Pro) from Russian 'bioregulator' research, promoted to support immunity in older people; evidence is mainly the developers' own small studies. Limited evidenceNot approved Supplement · Vitamins & minerals Magnesium glycinate A form of the mineral magnesium bound to the amino acid glycine, taken mainly for sleep and relaxation and to top up magnesium intake. Limited evidenceDietary supplement Supplement · Longevity NMN Nicotinamide mononucleotide, a building block the body uses to make NAD+, taken in hopes of slowing aging and boosting metabolism; human benefits are unproven. Limited evidenceDietary supplement Supplement · Longevity NR (nicotinamide riboside) A form of vitamin B3 that raises NAD+, a molecule cells use for energy, taken in hopes of healthier aging; trials show few clear health effects. Limited evidenceDietary supplement Peptide · Longevity MOTS-c A small peptide encoded in mitochondrial DNA, taken in hopes of improving metabolism, exercise capacity and aging; its effects have only been tested in rodents and cells.
