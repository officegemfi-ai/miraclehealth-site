Hormone balance

32 compounds studied for it · 18 with strong or moderate evidence

What the studies show for each compound people take for hormone balance, strongest evidence first, next to its Community Signal: what people who took it report. It isn’t a ranking of what to take: the evidence grade is for the compound as a whole, and each fact sheet says what it was studied for.

Studied for hormone balance

Medication · Hormone balance Anastrozole A prescription pill that stops the body making estrogen. Approved for hormone-sensitive breast cancer after menopause. Also studied in men with low testosterone, which it isn't approved for. Strong evidenceFDA-approved Medication · Hormone balance Cabergoline A prescription pill that lowers high levels of the hormone prolactin by acting like dopamine on the pituitary gland. Approved for high prolactin, including from pituitary tumors that make it. Strong evidenceFDA-approved Medication · Hormone balance Clomiphene A prescription fertility pill that triggers ovulation in women who don't ovulate regularly. Men also take it to raise their own testosterone, which it isn't approved for. Strong evidenceFDA-approved Hormone · Hormone balance Estradiol The body's strongest estrogen. Prescribed mainly to ease hot flashes and vaginal symptoms of menopause, and to prevent bone loss after it. Strong evidenceFDA-approved Medication · Hormone balance Exemestane A prescription pill that permanently blocks aromatase, the enzyme that makes estrogen. Approved for hormone-sensitive breast cancer after menopause. Studies in men are few and brief. Strong evidenceFDA-approved Hormone · Hormone balance FSH A prescription fertility hormone, made in the lab and given by injection. Approved to trigger ovulation and for IVF in women and, with hCG, to start sperm production in men. Strong evidenceFDA-approved Hormone · Hormone balance hCG A pregnancy hormone that acts like the hormone LH. Prescribed for men whose testicles are underactive because of a pituitary problem, for undescended testicles in boys, and to trigger ovulation. Strong evidenceFDA-approved Hormone · Hormone balance hMG A prescription fertility injection with FSH and LH activity, purified from the urine of women past menopause. Approved for IVF. Strong evidenceFDA-approved Hormone · Hormone balance Leuprolide A prescription injection that switches off the body's sex hormones. Sometimes taken for it

Studied mainly for something else, with some evidence or interest for hormone balance.

Medication · Skin & hair Dutasteride A prescription capsule that stops the body making the hormone DHT. Approved in the US for an enlarged prostate. Also used for male pattern hair loss, which it isn't approved for. Strong evidenceFDA-approved Medication · Skin & hair Finasteride A prescription pill that stops testosterone turning into the hormone DHT. Approved for male pattern hair loss and an enlarged prostate. Sexual and mood side effects are a known concern. Strong evidenceFDA-approved Peptide · Growth-hormone axis Tesamorelin A lab-made version of the brain hormone that triggers the release of growth hormone. FDA-approved to shrink excess deep belly fat in adults with HIV. Strong evidenceFDA-approved Medication · Skin & hair Spironolactone A prescription water pill for heart failure and high blood pressure. It also blocks male hormones, so it's used for acne and hair loss in women, which it isn't approved for. It’s context, not a direct comparison: in trials “improved” was measured on strict scales, while a member’s “better” is their own word. More on this.
