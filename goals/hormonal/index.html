Hormone balance

32 compounds studied for it · 18 with strong or moderate evidence

What the studies show for each compound people take for hormone balance, strongest evidence first, next to its Community Signal: what people who took it report. It isn’t a ranking of what to take: the evidence grade is for the compound as a whole, and each fact sheet says what it was studied for.
