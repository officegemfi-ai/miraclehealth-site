Studied for injury recovery & joints

Peptide · Healing & recovery BPC-157 A small piece of a protein first found in human stomach juice. People take it to heal tendons, muscles and the gut. Most of the evidence comes from rodents. Limited evidenceUnder FDA review Supplement · Immune & inflammation Curcumin The main yellow compound in turmeric, taken mostly for joint pain and inflammation. The evidence is still early, and turmeric supplements have been linked to rare liver injury. Limited evidenceDietary supplement Peptide · Healing & recovery GHK-Cu A small peptide that binds copper, found naturally in human blood. Used in skin creams and injected for skin, hair and healing. That rests mostly on small cosmetic studies and animal studies. Limited evidenceUnder FDA review Peptide · Healing & recovery Thymosin beta-4 A natural protein promoted for injury recovery, based on animal studies. In people it has been tested as eye drops and a skin gel, with mixed results. Not approved, and not the same thing as TB-500. Limited evidenceIn clinical trials Peptide · Healing & recovery Pentadeca Arginate A peptide described as a form of the experimental peptide BPC-157 (its arginine salt), used for injury healing. It has not been tested in published studies. Not FDA-approved. Would it have happened anyway?

Many people improve with time or on a placebo, whatever they take. In published trials:

  • 38%Other pain At least 30% reduction in pain intensity from baseline while on placebo Duarte GS 2022, Häuser W 2011, Derry S 2016, Moore RA 2010, Cepeda MS 2012
  • 35%Joint pain At least 30% reduction in WOMAC pain after 12 weeks on placebo pills. The low end is at least 50% relief on placebo pills (25%); the high end is at least 50% relief on a placebo gel (about 5 in 10). Moore RA 2010, Derry S 2016
  • 84%Injury healing Sustained recovery (pain 0–1 out of 10 for 7 days in a row) within 12 weeks on placebo. The low end is at least 50% pain relief after about a week on a placebo gel for strains and sprains ('2 or 3 in 10'). Williams CM 2014, Derry S 2015
  • 32%Tendons & ligaments Trial-defined 'success' with a wait-and-see approach at 6 weeks. The low end is complete recovery or much improvement after a placebo injection alone at 4 weeks; the high end is global improvement at 1 year without active treatment. Ikonen J 2022, Smidt N 2002, Coombes BK 2013

It’s context, not a direct comparison: in trials “improved” was measured on strict scales, while a member’s “better” is their own word. More on this.
