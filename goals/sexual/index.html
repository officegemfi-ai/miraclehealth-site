Sexual health & libido

8 compounds studied for it · 4 with strong or moderate evidence

What the studies show for each compound people take for sexual health & libido, strongest evidence first, next to its Community Signal: what people who took it report. It isn’t a ranking of what to take: the evidence grade is for the compound as a whole, and each fact sheet says what it was studied for.

Studied for sexual health & libido

Peptide · Sexual health PT-141 A lab-made peptide that activates melanocortin receptors, FDA-approved as Vyleesi for low sexual desire in premenopausal women and also used off-label for libido and erections. Strong evidenceFDA-approved Medication · Sexual health Sildenafil A prescription PDE5-inhibitor pill for erectile dysfunction (Viagra); the same drug is also sold as Revatio for pulmonary arterial hypertension. Strong evidenceFDA-approved Medication · Sexual health Tadalafil A prescription PDE5-inhibitor pill for erectile dysfunction and for urinary symptoms of an enlarged prostate; in trials, its effect on erections lasted up to 36 hours. Strong evidenceFDA-approved Hormone · Hormone balance Testosterone A male sex hormone (androgen) prescribed to men with low testosterone from testicular, pituitary or hypothalamic problems; a Schedule III controlled substance in the US. Strong evidenceFDA-approved Hormone · Hormone balance DHEA A hormone made by the adrenal glands that the body converts to testosterone and estrogen; sold as a supplement for libido, well-being and aging, with weak evidence. Limited evidenceDietary supplement Peptide · Sexual health Kisspeptin-10 The smallest fully active fragment of kisspeptin, a natural hormone that switches on the reproductive hormone chain; taken to raise testosterone and for sexual health. Limited evidenceFDA safety-risk list Peptide · Hormone balance Testagen A Russian dietary-supplement peptide from Khavinson's 'bioregulator' research, promoted for male reproductive health and testosterone; human evidence is one small study by its developers. Limited evidenceNot approved Supplement · Hormone balance Tongkat ali Root extract of the Southeast Asian shrubby tree Eurycoma longifolia, long used as an aphrodisiac and taken mainly to raise testosterone; evidence comes from a few small trials. Limited evidenceDietary supplement

Sometimes taken for it

Studied mainly for something else, with some evidence or interest for sexual health & libido.

Medication · Hormone balance Clomiphene A prescription fertility pill that triggers ovulation in women who don't ovulate regularly; it is also used off-label in men to raise their own testosterone. Strong evidenceFDA-approved Hormone · Hormone balance Estradiol The body's most potent estrogen, prescribed mainly to relieve menopausal hot flashes and vaginal symptoms and to prevent bone loss after menopause. Strong evidenceFDA-approved Hormone · Hormone balance FSH A prescription fertility hormone injection, made by recombinant DNA technology, approved for ovulation induction and IVF in women and, with hCG, to start sperm production in men. Strong evidenceFDA-approved Hormone · Hormone balance hMG A prescription fertility injection of FSH and LH activity purified from postmenopausal women's urine, approved for IVF; it is used off-label in men to start sperm production. Strong evidenceFDA-approved Medication · Hormone balance Enclomiphene One of the two forms in the fertility drug clomiphene, studied as a pill to raise men's own testosterone; it is not approved in the US or Europe. Moderate evidenceCompounded by prescription Supplement · Mood & stress Ashwagandha An Ayurvedic herb (Withania somnifera) whose root extracts are taken mainly to ease stress and anxiety and to improve sleep. Limited evidenceDietary supplement Supplement · Energy & mitochondria CoQ10 A substance the body makes that helps cells produce energy, taken for heart health, statin-related muscle aches, migraine and energy. Limited evidenceDietary supplement Hormone · Hormone balance Gonadorelin A lab-made copy of the brain hormone GnRH, which tells the pituitary to release LH and FSH; men take it to support testicular function, with little evidence. Limited evidenceCompounded by prescription Peptide · Skin & hair Melanotan II An unapproved lab-made hormone analog injected for tanning and sometimes for sexual arousal, linked in case reports to mole changes and serious side effects. Limited evidenceUnder FDA review Peptide · Hormone balance Prostamax A short lab-made peptide (Lys-Glu-Asp-Pro) from Russian 'bioregulator' research, promoted for prostate health; published evidence is limited to lab studies on cells and DNA. Animal and lab studiesNot approved
