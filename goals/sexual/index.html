Sexual health & libido.

8 compounds studied for it · 4 with strong or moderate evidence

What the studies show for each compound people take for sexual health & libido, strongest evidence first, next to its Community Signal: what people who took it report. It isn’t a ranking of what to take: the evidence grade is for the compound as a whole, and each fact sheet says what it was studied for.

Studied for sexual health & libido

Peptide · Sexual health PT-141 A lab-made peptide, FDA-approved as Vyleesi for low sexual desire in women before menopause. Also used for libido and erections, which it isn't approved for. Strong evidenceFDA-approved Medication · Sexual health Sildenafil The prescription pill in Viagra (a PDE5 inhibitor), for erectile dysfunction. The same drug is also sold as Revatio for pulmonary arterial hypertension (high blood pressure in the lungs). Strong evidenceFDA-approved Medication · Sexual health Tadalafil A prescription pill (a PDE5 inhibitor) for erectile dysfunction and for urinary symptoms of an enlarged prostate. In trials, its effect on erections lasted up to 36 hours. Strong evidenceFDA-approved Hormone · Hormone balance Testosterone A male sex hormone. Prescribed to men whose testosterone is low because of a problem with the testicles, the pituitary gland or the hypothalamus. A Schedule III controlled substance in the US. Strong evidenceFDA-approved Hormone · Hormone balance DHEA A hormone your adrenal glands make, which the body turns into testosterone and estrogen. Sold as a supplement for libido, well-being and aging. The evidence is weak. Limited evidenceDietary supplement Peptide · Sexual health Kisspeptin-10 The smallest fully active piece of kisspeptin, a natural hormone that switches on the body's chain of reproductive hormones. Taken to raise testosterone and for sexual health. Limited evidenceFDA safety-risk list Peptide · Hormone balance Testagen A Russian supplement peptide from Khavinson's 'bioregulator' research, promoted for male reproductive health and testosterone. The evidence in people is one small study by its developers. Limited evidenceNot approved Supplement · Hormone balance Tongkat ali Root extract of a Southeast Asian tree (Eurycoma longifolia), long used as an aphrodisiac and taken mainly to raise testosterone. The evidence comes from a few small trials. Limited evidenceDietary supplement

Sometimes taken for it

Studied mainly for something else, with some evidence or interest for sexual health & libido.

Medication · Hormone balance Clomiphene A prescription fertility pill that triggers ovulation in women who don't ovulate regularly. Men also take it to raise their own testosterone, which it isn't approved for. Strong evidenceFDA-approved Hormone · Hormone balance Estradiol The body's strongest estrogen. Prescribed mainly to ease hot flashes and vaginal symptoms of menopause, and to prevent bone loss after it. Strong evidenceFDA-approved Hormone · Hormone balance FSH A prescription fertility hormone, made in the lab and given by injection. Approved to trigger ovulation and for IVF in women and, with hCG, to start sperm production in men. Strong evidenceFDA-approved Hormone · Hormone balance hMG A prescription fertility injection with FSH and LH activity, purified from the urine of women past menopause. Approved for IVF. Also used in men to start sperm production, which it isn't approved for. Strong evidenceFDA-approved Medication · Hormone balance Enclomiphene One of the two forms that make up the fertility drug clomiphene. Studied as a pill to raise men's own testosterone. Not approved in the US or Europe. Moderate evidenceCompounded by prescription Supplement · Mood & stress Ashwagandha An herb from Ayurvedic medicine (Withania somnifera). Extracts of its root are taken mainly to ease stress and anxiety and to sleep better. Limited evidenceDietary supplement Supplement · Energy & mitochondria CoQ10 A substance your body makes that helps cells produce energy. Taken for heart health, muscle aches from statins, migraine and energy. Limited evidenceDietary supplement Hormone · Hormone balance Gonadorelin A lab-made copy of GnRH, the brain hormone that tells the pituitary gland to release LH and FSH. Men take it to support the testicles. There is little evidence behind that. Limited evidenceCompounded by prescription Peptide · Skin & hair Melanotan II An unapproved, lab-made version of a hormone, injected for tanning and sometimes for sexual arousal. Case reports link it to mole changes and serious side effects. Limited evidenceUnder FDA review Peptide · Hormone balance Prostamax A short lab-made peptide from Russian 'bioregulator' research, promoted for prostate health. The published evidence is limited to lab studies on cells and DNA. Animal and lab studiesNot approved
