Studied for skin, hair & appearance

Medication · Skin & hair Dutasteride A prescription capsule that stops the body making the hormone DHT. Approved in the US for an enlarged prostate. Also used for male pattern hair loss, which it isn't approved for. Strong evidenceFDA-approved Medication · Skin & hair Finasteride A prescription pill that stops testosterone turning into the hormone DHT. Approved for male pattern hair loss and an enlarged prostate. Sexual and mood side effects are a known concern. Strong evidenceFDA-approved Peptide · Skin & hair Melanotan I (afamelanotide) A lab-made version of alpha-MSH, the hormone that darkens skin. Approved as an implant for a rare disorder that makes people sensitive to light. Unlicensed Melanotan I products are used for tanning. Strong evidenceFDA-approved Medication · Skin & hair Minoxidil Sold over the counter as a scalp treatment for hereditary hair loss, and by prescription as a tablet for severe high blood pressure. Low-dose tablets are also used for hair, which they aren't approved for. Strong evidenceOver the counter Medication · Skin & hair Tretinoin A prescription skin medicine for acne. One cream version is also approved to soften fine wrinkles, blotchy pigment and roughness on the face. Strong evidenceFDA-approved Medication · Skin & hair Spironolactone A prescription water pill for heart failure and high blood pressure. It also blocks male hormones, so it's used for acne and hair loss in women, which it isn't approved for. Moderate evidenceFDA-approved Supplement · Skin & hair Collagen peptides Collagen broken down into small pieces of protein. Taken by mouth, mainly for skin hydration, elasticity and wrinkles, and sometimes for joint symptoms. Limited evidenceDietary supplement Peptide · Healing & recovery GHK-Cu A small peptide that binds copper, found naturally in human blood. Used in skin creams and injected for skin, hair and healing. That rests mostly on small cosmetic studies and animal studies. Limited evidenceUnder FDA review Peptide · Liver & detox Glutathione An antioxidant your body makes. Taken by mouth or by IV for liver health, detox and skin lightening. Studies in people are small, and IV use has caused serious reactions. Limited evidenceCompounded by prescription Peptide · Skin & hair Melanotan II An unapproved, lab-made version of a hormone, injected for tanning and sometimes for sexual arousal. Case reports link it to mole changes and serious side effects. Limited evidenceUnder FDA review Peptide · Skin & hair SNAP-8 (acetyl octapeptide-3) A small lab-made peptide used in anti-wrinkle skin products. Marketed as a skin-applied alternative to Botox for expression lines. Limited evidenceNot approved
