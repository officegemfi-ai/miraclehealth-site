Skin, hair & appearance

11 compounds studied for it · 6 with strong or moderate evidence

What the studies show for each compound people take for skin, hair & appearance, strongest evidence first, next to its Community Signal: what people who took it report. It isn’t a ranking of what to take: the evidence grade is for the compound as a whole, and each fact sheet says what it was studied for.

Studied for skin, hair & appearance

Medication · Skin & hair Dutasteride A prescription capsule that blocks DHT production, approved in the US for an enlarged prostate; it is used off-label for male pattern hair loss. Strong evidenceFDA-approved Medication · Skin & hair Finasteride A prescription pill that blocks the conversion of testosterone to DHT, approved for male pattern hair loss and enlarged prostate; sexual and mood side effects are a known concern. Strong evidenceFDA-approved Peptide · Skin & hair Melanotan I (afamelanotide) A lab-made analog of the skin-darkening hormone alpha-MSH, approved as an implant for a rare light-sensitivity disorder; unlicensed Melanotan I products are used for tanning. Strong evidenceFDA-approved Medication · Skin & hair Minoxidil Minoxidil is an over-the-counter scalp treatment for hereditary hair loss and a prescription tablet for severe high blood pressure; low-dose tablets are used off-label for hair. Strong evidenceOver the counter Medication · Skin & hair Tretinoin A prescription skin medicine for acne; one cream version is also approved to soften fine wrinkles, blotchy pigment and roughness of facial skin. Strong evidenceFDA-approved Medication · Skin & hair Spironolactone A prescription water pill for heart failure and high blood pressure that also blocks male hormones, so it is used off-label for acne and hair loss in women. Moderate evidenceFDA-approved Supplement · Skin & hair Collagen peptides Collagen broken down into small protein fragments, taken by mouth mainly to improve skin hydration, elasticity and wrinkles, and sometimes for joint symptoms. Limited evidenceDietary supplement Peptide · Healing & recovery GHK-Cu A small copper-binding peptide found naturally in human blood, used in skin creams and injected for skin, hair and healing, based mostly on small cosmetic and animal studies. Limited evidenceUnder FDA review Peptide · Liver & detox Glutathione An antioxidant the body makes, taken by mouth or IV for liver health, detox and skin lightening; human studies are small and IV use has caused serious reactions. Limited evidenceCompounded by prescription Peptide · Skin & hair Melanotan II An unapproved lab-made hormone analog injected for tanning and sometimes for sexual arousal, linked in case reports to mole changes and serious side effects. Limited evidenceUnder FDA review Peptide · Skin & hair SNAP-8 (acetyl octapeptide-3) A lab-made eight-amino-acid peptide used in anti-wrinkle skin products, marketed as a topical alternative to Botox for expression lines.
