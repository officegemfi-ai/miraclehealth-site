Questions

Straight answers.

Every weight and rule is on How the numbers are made. Something else? Write to hello@miraclehealth.us

What counts as a report?

A member’s own words about something they took: what it was for, how it went compared with before (better, about the same or worse) and for how long. All three count, so a report that it did nothing moves the number too.

Why does a Signal start from public forums?

Most compounds don’t have many member reports yet. So every Signal starts from Unhyped’s reading of public forums, which counts as 50 reports, and every first-hand report here moves it toward members’ own experience. Its level says how far along it is, from Forum data to Signal Certified.

What does Sig do with my report?

It reads how it went, for how long, what it was for and any side effects, then shows you that read in one line: “Did we read you right?” You confirm it or fix any part with a tap. Your word always wins over Sig’s read.

Why does it ask again at 2, 6 and 12 weeks?

Because how something goes can change. Each time you’re asked “How is it now?”: better, about the same or worse, still taking it or not, and if not, why. The latest answer is the one that counts, and why people stop is counted too.

How is the score worked out?

It’s a number from 0 to 100 with four parts: how reports went (65%), how much they agree (15%), how many independent voices there are (10%) and how much there is to go on (10%). How the numbers are made has every weight.

Does every report count the same?

Reports are weighted, never people. One its author confirmed, one from someone who took it longer and one followed up with check-ins count for more. Each account counts once per compound, with its latest report, and nobody gets a credibility score.

Can anyone push a number up?

Not by buying it or flooding it. There’s no shop, no ads and no sellers, and buying talk is taken down. When five or more new accounts report on one compound within 48 hours, what each of them adds counts a quarter until a moderator has looked, and copied posts are held out.

Does a high Signal mean it works?

No. It’s what people report, not proof: many people feel better with time or on a placebo, whatever they take. Next to what members report for a use, Unhyped shows how many people improved anyway in published trials, and what the studies show is in each compound’s fact sheet, kept apart.

How often does it change?

It’s checked every minute and kept as a new version whenever it changes. The website, Ask Sig and the Library all read the same version.

Where do the numbers come from?

The studies come from FDA documents, NIH fact sheets and published trials, and each claim links to its source. The Community Signal is read from public forums for now, and shifts to members’ own reports as they add up. How the numbers are made walks through every step.

Who are Sig’s twelve agents?

Sig is the AI behind Ask Sig. Its agents each do one job: the Safety agent reads every question first and checks every answer last; the Planner turns a goal into what studies measure; the Recommender reads the fact sheets and writes the answer; the Evidence librarian grades each study; the Relatives and Mechanism agents say what close relatives and biology suggest, marked as such; the Combination agent and the Synergy auditor look at what’s taken together; the Markers agent names blood tests worth asking about; the Stack builder lays out options to discuss; the community brings in what people report. The Interpretation agent reasons it all through, for efficacy alone, last.

Is this medical advice?

No. It’s published research and people’s experiences, always labeled as which is which. Talk to your doctor before you start, stop or change anything.

Why won’t you tell me a dose?

Because the right dose depends on you, your health and what else you take. For many of these there is no tested dose in people at all. That’s a conversation for your doctor, and Sig can help you write the questions to bring.

Do you sell anything?

No. There is nothing to buy here: no shop, no ads, no affiliate links. Posts that name sellers or say where to buy are taken down.

What happens to what I share?

You sign in to the community with your phone number, and nobody else ever sees it. Only signed-in members can read posts, unless you choose to make one of your reports public. You can delete your account and everything you wrote at any time. Sig’s chat needs no account, and we don’t store what you write there. We don’t sell your information, show ads or use tracking cookies. Read the privacy notice.

Is it free?

Yes. Reading the Library, asking Sig and joining the community are all free. The community is for adults 18 and over.
