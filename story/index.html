~/story · the new Miracle Health

One app.
Twelve agents.
Every answer, checked.

Miracle Health is now one app. The Library, Ask Sig, the community and My Miracle each open as a window in Sig’s terminal, side by side, the same everywhere. Behind every answer are twelve agents, each with one job, each signing what it made.

Ask Sig a questionOpen the Library

01 · the twelve

Meet the twelve.

Eleven gather what’s known, each in its own colour round Sig’s ring. The twelfth sits at its centre and reasons it all through, last, strictly for efficacy. Colour on Miracle Health is a signature: wherever you see one, that agent made it.

  1. 01

    Safety agent

    Reads your message first, and checks every answer before you see it.

  2. 02

    Planner

    Turns your goal into what studies measure. “Lose fat” becomes body fat, waist and lean mass.

  3. 03

    Recommender

    Finds the options in the Library and reads their fact sheets, with what you’ve told it. It never asks you questions.

  4. 04

    Evidence librarian

    Finds every study result on exactly that: how strong it is, and who was studied.

  5. 05

    Relatives agent

    Finds things that work through the same hormone or receptor, like testosterone for enclomiphene, and says whose evidence it is.

  6. 06

    Mechanism agent

    Explains how each one could affect your goal, from its fact sheet. That is theory, not a test result, and it says so.

  7. 07

    The community

    What members report first-hand and what public forums say, as totals. It leads where the studies run out.

  8. 08

    Combination agent

    Says whether the things you take together overlap or work in different ways.

  9. 09

    Markers agent

    Flags blood tests that two things push in opposite directions, like IGF-1 with HGH and enclomiphene.

  10. 10

    Synergy auditor

    Checks everything together with your medicines and conditions.

  11. 11

    Stack builder

    Lays out what you take and the options to discuss with your doctor. Never doses, never sellers.

  12. 12

    Interpretation agent

    Runs last. Reasons through everything the others found, strictly about efficacy: how likely it is to work for what you asked, how big the effect could be, and how sure anyone can be.

03 · prints

Every compound has a print.

A print is a fact sheet drawn as one mark, so you can tell two compounds apart, and compare them, at a glance. Nothing in it is decoration.

  • Spokes run to the places in the body the sheet names: the body’s 38 places, head to toe, clockwise from the top.
  • Beads are its lines there: acts raises lowers blocks worth a blood test a caution, cautions outermost.
  • The core is the family’s shape (peptide, hormone, medication, supplement) and the evidence inside it, ring by ring, in the Evidence librarian’s blue.
  • The edge carries the Community Signal in lime, once it’s known.

05 · what never changes

The rules are in the status bar.

At the foot of every window, always: safety on; no doses, no sellers, no diagnosis. They aren’t settings. They’re how it’s built.

SIGonlinesafety onno doses · no sellers · no diagnosis
  • No doses. Ever, for anything. The right amount is a conversation with your doctor.
  • No sellers. No shop, no ads, no affiliate links. Nobody pays to be in the Library.
  • No diagnosis. Sig explains evidence. It doesn’t tell you what you have or what to take.
  • Your chats stay with you. On this device until you delete them, never on our servers.
